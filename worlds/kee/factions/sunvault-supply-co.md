# Sunvault Supply Co.

A large material supplier based in an unnamed tropical continent. It sent [Kessik Sunscale](../characters/npcs/kessik-sunscale.md) to [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md) to see in person whether [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) is as good a building material as people say, and whether it is useful and viable to stock. The company has only heard third or fourth hand accounts of the wood from sailors.

## Not recorded

- The continent it is based in (the owner has not named it yet).
- Leadership, size, and its products.

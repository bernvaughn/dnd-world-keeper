# The Den-Keepers

A faction of particularly zealous [Ursine](../species/ursine.md) in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md) who oppose the digging and export of [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) (Den-oak), which they treat as sacred. Mostly intimidating, and they don't usually need to fight, though they will if pushed.

## Methods and members

- They usually intimidate rather than fight. If the players tip the scales to violence, the fight is real.
- [Grask Ironmaw](../characters/npcs/grask-ironmaw.md) is the one Ursine among them who is a real threat in combat.
- Their leader, [Orsa Quillmane](../characters/npcs/orsa-quillmane.md), is willing to negotiate if the players resist.
- They are after specific pieces of name-ridged Den-oak mixed in with the shipment, which are of great cultural value to the Ursine.

## Proposed, not confirmed

- They are not against all Castori; they want the digging stopped.
- [Frostden](../continents/berksheland/cities/frostden/README.md) might privately sympathize with them without admitting it.

## Not recorded

- Size, base, and how they get their information.
- Their relationship to [Lord Butterclaw](../characters/npcs/lord-butterclaw.md) and to [Hesper Voss](../characters/npcs/hesper-voss.md).

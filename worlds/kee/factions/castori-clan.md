# Castori Clan

The formal home of the [Castori](../species/castori.md) in [Xianhua](../continents/xianhua/README.md): essentially the noble house of the beavers there. Its lodges include the Floodedfield den (also written "Floodfield"; both spellings are in use, so watch for which one sticks), which raised [Ryu Boku](../characters/pcs/ryu-boku.md).

[Ryu Boku](../characters/pcs/ryu-boku.md) was exiled for training in secret as a samurai. If he survives the cold of the north and proves himself worthy, he will be allowed to join the Castori Clan formally and be given his den name back.

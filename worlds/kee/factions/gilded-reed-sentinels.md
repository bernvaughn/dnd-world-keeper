# Gilded Reed Sentinels


[Murkwallow](../continents/sead/cities/murkwallow/README.md)'s most respected and feared defensive force. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

Veteran bullywug marsh-wardens, disciplined grung skirmishers, and a smaller cadre of sacred silver-shield officers, sworn to protect the city's rulers, vaults, barge approaches, and mithril works. Not a standing army in the dryland sense: a swamp-adapted elite defense corps, comfortable in courtly ceremony, silent canal pursuit, riot suppression, and lethal ambush in knee-deep blackwater.

## Ranks

1. **Reedling:** new recruit or city watch auxiliary
2. **Marshguard:** full Sentinel soldier
3. **Mirestriker:** veteran skirmisher, often grung
4. **Lantern Sergeant:** squad leader
5. **Silverback Warden:** senior officer and elite guardian
6. **Keeper of the Channels:** regional commander of approach routes and water defenses
7. **High Sentinel of Murkwallow:** overall commander; [Vorru-Kesh Blackreed](../characters/npcs/vorru-kesh-blackreed.md)

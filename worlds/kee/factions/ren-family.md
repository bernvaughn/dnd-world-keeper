The wealthiest family in [Ardmont](../continents/sead/cities/ardmont/README.md) and on the island of Sead. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

- Has a large home in Ardmont.
- Their [estate](../continents/sead/cities/bridges/locations/the-ren-estate.md) is at 43 Silverspan Lane, Nobles District, Bridges.
- Members are not recorded.

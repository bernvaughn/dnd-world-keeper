# Gnawfast Excavation Co.

The company that runs the bulk of the [Castori](../species/castori.md) operations in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md), digging up [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) from under the ice. Its work is the source of the tension between the Castori and the [Ursine](../species/ursine.md). Its company town is [Longdam](../continents/berksheland/cities/longdam/README.md).

# Gnawfast Excavation Co.

The company that runs the bulk of the [Castori](../species/castori.md) operations in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md), digging up [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) from under the ice. Its work is the source of the tension between the Castori and the [Ursine](../species/ursine.md).

## Notes

- Spelling: the owner typed "Gnawdast" once; "Gnawfast" is the spelling used here. Both variants kept until the owner confirms.
- Leadership, headquarters, size, and how it relates to the Castori lodges or to Ardmont: not recorded.

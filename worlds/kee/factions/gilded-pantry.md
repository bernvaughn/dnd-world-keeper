# The Gilded Pantry

A crime syndicate made up of halflings. [Veralyn Fastfeet](../characters/pcs/veralyn-fastfeet.md) stole a beautiful set of leather armor from the personal stash of its matron, [Auntie Plumsworth](../characters/npcs/auntie-plumsworth.md), without realizing who they were, and is now on the run from them aboard [The Stubborn Tern](../ships/the-stubborn-tern.md), bound for [Coldanchor](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md).

## Proposed (not established)

- A smuggling and fencing outfit with a respectable front, nominally a grocer's guild. Proposed by the assistant in session 0; the owner chose the name only.

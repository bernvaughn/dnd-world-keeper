# Applewood Families


The three halfling families of [Applewood Veil](../continents/sead/locations/applewood-veil.md): Copperrabbit, Applefound, and Heartbrand. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

- **Copperrabbit:** Sharmin Copperrabbit is mayor.
- **Heartbrand:** Rister Heartbrand is sheriff.
- **Applefound:** no members are named.

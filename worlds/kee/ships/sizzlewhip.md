# Sizzlewhip

A gnomish ship captained by [Brindle](../characters/npcs/brindle-nackle.md) of [Saltmaril](../continents/sead/cities/saltmaril/README.md). It carried the party to [Ember Reef Island](../continents/sead/locations/ember-reef-island.md), listing badly from a ruined keel, and was repaired on the island. Brindle revealed afterward that it could fly the entire time. From Discord messages. See [session-2025-07-22-dm2](../sessions/session-2025-07-22-dm2.md).

# The Stubborn Tern

A ship that arrives at [Coldanchor](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md) in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md). Player characters who are not native to the continent may arrive on it. [Kessik Sunscale](../characters/npcs/kessik-sunscale.md) may be a passenger.

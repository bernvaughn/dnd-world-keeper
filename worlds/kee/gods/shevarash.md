# Shevarash, the Night Hunter

- **Tier:** Seed God
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Domains:** War

God of elves and revenge. He found this realm and desires to create a world without Lolth's influence; he is constantly consumed by thoughts of retribution and bitterness. His church is tiny but rigidly disciplined: clerics drill, design tactics, and map the Upper Underdark, expecting to find drow territories. They pray at dusk; the holiest day is Midwinter night, when the Dark Court Slaughter is remembered and new clerics scream vows of vengeance, swearing neither to laugh nor smile until the Spider Queen and her followers are no more.

- **Dogma:** "Redemption and revenge may be achieved through the utter destruction of the drow and the dark powers they serve. Only then may the joy of life begin anew. Hunt fearlessly!"

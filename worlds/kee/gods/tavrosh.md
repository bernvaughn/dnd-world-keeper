# Tavrosh, the Rolling Cask

- **Tier:** Minor God
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Domains:** Trickery, Peace

Minor God of Ale, Revels, Open Tables, and Tipsy Fellowship. Depicted as a tortle: broad, sea-worn, his great rounded shell carved and painted like a festival cask, banded in bronze and draped with strings of mugs, beads, shells, and little bells; he wears a patchwork vest of tavern banners and ship flags and carries a massive oaken tankard. Also titled Shell of the Last Round, The Laugh Beneath the Lanterns, Keeper of the Open Mug, and "Uncle Tav."

He governs alcohol and brewing, parties and feasts, hospitality and shared tables, social courage and communal joy; nobody should celebrate alone.

- **Symbol:** a frothing tankard painted on a tortle shell (alternately, a round turtle shell patterned like a barrel top).
- **Sacred animals:** river turtles and sea turtles.
- **Sacred drink:** Shellfoam Ale (also Lantern Rum, Moonwine, Mossback Mead).
- **Clergy:** Caskbearers, Shellpours, Lantern Hosts, and Keepers of the Last Round.
- **Rituals:** The First Pour, The Empty Chair, The Rolling Toast, The Last Round Benediction.
- **Favored weapon:** club or quarterstaff.
- **Shrines:** appear in taverns, breweries, and docks, marked "Sit. Eat. Stay."
- **Prayer:** "Rolling Cask, keep our cups from drying, / our doors from closing, / our songs from thinning, / and our hearts from hardening. / Let strangers sit as strangers no longer."

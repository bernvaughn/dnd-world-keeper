# Korthar, the Flame of Anarchy

- **Tier:** True God
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Domains:** Life, Light, Chaos, War

Also titled The Living Chaos and Warlord of the Eternal Blaze. Symbol: a blazing sun surrounded by shattered chains, with a blood-red sword piercing through the center. He manifests as a towering figure of shifting, incandescent flame.

His view of life is twisted: life is fuel for the eternal cycle of war and destruction; his light is the searing light of a world in turmoil; chaos is the heart of his existence, and order is a prison; war is the ultimate expression of life.

- **Dogma:** *Fight until the end. Embrace chaos. War is divine. Life as fuel.*
- **Clergy:** warriors first, priests second. His temples are more akin to war camps, and rituals often involve blood sacrifices of soldiers or prisoners of war.
- **Powers:** Flame of Anarchy, Searing Light, Chaotic Manifestation, Warcry of Korthar.

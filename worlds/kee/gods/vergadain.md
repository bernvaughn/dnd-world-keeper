# Vergadain, the Merchant King

- **Tier:** Seed God
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Domains:** Trickery

God of dwarves, luck, trickery, negotiation, and wealth, who delights in the art of the deal so long as it turns a profit. His clerics are expected to be personally wealthy while keeping the local temple filled with treasure. Holy days are coin festivals, held on the days before and after a full moon, on Greengrass, and on any day declared favorable by the Merchant Prince.

Very little is known about him: after his dwarves sealed themselves off from the world 2,000 years ago after the Leviathan Wars, much of dwarven lore and history is lost to time or considered myth.

# Nangnang

- **Tier:** Seed God
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Domains:** not stated

The grung trickster god from the Forgotten Realms, one of the nine trickster gods of Omu: a primal grung spirit whose teaching is "serve only yourself," greedy and skittish, who stole the goodly Shagambi's spear. Her brother is [Ghurm](ghurm.md), the bullywugs' god of the marsh-works; the two split the amphibian peoples between them: hers are the frogs, his are the toads.

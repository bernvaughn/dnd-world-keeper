# Thal'ria, the Serene Passage

- **Tier:** True God
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Domains:** Death, Twilight, Grave, Peace

Portfolio: death, sleep, rest, comfort. Symbol: a crescent moon over a field of soft lavender, with a small silver bird resting on the moon's curve. A protector of the peaceful passage of life and death, revered by those seeking comfort in their final moments; she ensures death is a noble, gentle end to a well-lived life. She resides in the Serene Meadows, where souls who have lived noble, kind lives rest in eternal peace.

- **Tenets:** Peaceful Rest; Noble Endings; Release of Pain; Sanctuary of Sleep; Eternal Peace.
- **Rituals:** the Offering of the Restful Slumber, the Final Night Vigil, Dream Offerings, the Transition Rite.
- **Worshippers:** hospice workers and caregivers, grief counselors and mourners, and scholars and philosophers of mortality.

# Ghurm, the Unblinking Steward

- **Tier:** Minor God
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Domains:** Order, Forge

The bullywugs' god, made for Sead: god of labor, vigilance, and the marsh-works. The dikes, canals, reed farms, and mines of [Murkwallow](../continents/sead/cities/murkwallow/README.md) are his temples, and work done well is his only liturgy. Depicted as an immense bullywug in sentinel's armor standing motionless in the shallows, a workman's hammer in one hand and a bundle of cut reeds in the other, eyes never blinking; his symbol is an unblinking eye over two crossed reeds.

- **Tenets:** *The marsh feeds those who maintain it. A duty accepted is a duty finished. Idle hands flood the village. Watch, work, endure.*
- **Clergy:** the Reedwardens, part priest and part works-foreman.
- **Holy day:** the annual Repair of the Great Dike, worked, not celebrated. He has no festivals.
- **Family:** brother of [Nangnang](nangnang.md), the grung trickster: the two split the amphibian peoples between them. His are the toads, stern and hard-working like him; hers are the frogs.

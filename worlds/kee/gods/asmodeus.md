# Asmodeus

- **Tier:** True God
- **Alignment:** not stated
- **Domains:** not stated

A Forgotten Realms power seated among Sead's original True Gods: the exception among them. [Akira "B.D.E." Hornraven](../characters/npcs/akira-hornraven.md)'s god. Akira's loyalty is to Asmodeus, his holy symbol is Asmodeus's, and Asmodeus sent him from another realm to confront the growing Graz'zt threat.

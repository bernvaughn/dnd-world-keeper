# Xalithar, the Maker of Truth

- **Tier:** Minor God
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Domains:** Knowledge, Forge

God of teaching and advancement. Symbol: a golden anvil with an open book upon it, surrounded by sparks of light. Depicted as an ageless, gender-neutral figure with silver skin and robes of shifting blueprint patterns, hands stained with ink or metalwork. Patron of blacksmiths, engineers, inventors, and scholars; his forge is also the forge of ideas.

- **Holy text:** the Codex of Creation.
- **Cleric titles:** Smiths of Truth, Craftsmen of Knowledge, Minds of the Forge.
- **Rituals:** the Forging of Knowledge, the Day of Progress, the Mind-Forging Vigil.
- **Tenets:** Progress Is Patient; Share What You Learn; The Craft Is Ever-Refining; Never Stop Creating.

# Yondalla, The Blessed One

- **Tier:** Seed God
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Domains:** Life

Creator of the halfling race and matriarch of not only the halfling pantheon, but the whole race itself; god of fertility and protection. With her charming personality and friendly demeanor she is an example to all halflings, who almost all share her curiosity, loyalty, and sense of mischief.

- **Basic prayer:** "Fill my cup with plenty, mother. May it never run dry."

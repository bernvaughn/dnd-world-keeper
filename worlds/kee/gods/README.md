# The Pantheon of Sead

The gods are broken down primarily into four major categories. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

- **True Gods:** the prime powers, having 4 domains each.
- **Minor Gods:** with 2 domains each.
- **Demi-Gods:** with 1 domain; usually a storied mortal that elevated to godhood.
- **Seed Gods:** gods from other realms that have influence here.

The pantheon's domain lists are still incomplete: several entries say only "Domains include..." or "not stated," with the rest unassigned. The source refers to an "Open Threads" chapter for this, which was not in the document.

Gruumsh (the orc god banished from this world), Lolth (the Spider Queen), Graz'zt (a demon lord and growing threat), and Garl Glittergold (the gnome god of the Forgotten Realms) are mentioned in the entries but are not listed as gods of the pantheon.

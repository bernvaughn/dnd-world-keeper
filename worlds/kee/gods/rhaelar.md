# Rhaelar, The Steel Binder

- **Tier:** True God
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Domains:** Order, War, Knowledge, Arcana

Also titled The Iron Marshal and Keeper of Codes. Symbol: a sword crossed with a quill, bound by chains of silver. God of mercenaries, soldiers, and mages who follow a strict code of honor and order; he governs those who fight for coin, knowledge, or duty according to the letter of a code.

- **The Code of the Steel Binder:** Honor Above All; Discipline in Battle; Respect for Knowledge; Loyalty to the Contract; Victory Through Strategy; The Shield of Order.
- **Temples:** fortresses of discipline and training, often in military strongholds or universities.
- **Favored weapon:** the Steelbinder Sword, a longsword of silver-steel said to have been forged by Rhaelar's own hand.

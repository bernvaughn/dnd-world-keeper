# Luthic, the Great Orc Mother

- **Tier:** Seed God
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Domains:** Life, Nature

The orc goddess of caves, mate of Gruumsh, patron of female orcs, healing, and home; "the Cave Mother," the maternal paragon and enduring bedrock of orc culture. She appears as an 8.5-foot-tall female orc with dark brown skin and black claws 4 feet long, and is thought to take the guise of a giant cave bear.

Her son [Vreldrul Dakh](vreldrul-dakh.md) banished Gruumsh; without Gruumsh's crushing influence Luthic has softened some, but still pushes her kind to prove themselves the strongest warriors around, never letting their guard down should Gruumsh reawaken in this realm. Her clerics pray when the moon is highest; her holy days tie to the moon, and the Feast of the Moon is celebrated as the Coming of the Winter Cave.

# Kurtulmak

- **Tier:** Seed God
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Domains:** Law, Trickery, War

The kobolds' patron from the Forgotten Realms: god of war, mining, and trapmaking, a steel-scaled giant kobold with a stinger tail. In the Realms he is the sworn enemy of the gnome god Garl Glittergold, whose prank collapsed Kurtulmak's great hall; Sead's gnomes follow Callarduran, and no such feud is recorded here.

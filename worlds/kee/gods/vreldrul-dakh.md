# Vreldrul Dakh

- **Tier:** Demi-God
- **Alignment:** not stated
- **Domains:** Order ("Domains include Order")

Son of [Luthic](luthic.md), Banisher of Gruumsh: the historic orc hero who fought Gruumsh in single combat and banished his evil influence from this world. The nation of [Vreldrul Dakh](../continents/sead/locations/vreldrul-dakh.md) is named for him.

# Ursine

Bipedal bear-folk, native to [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md), where they live in relatively high numbers. Playable. There is tension between the Ursine and the [Castori](castori.md), who are expanding their digging of [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) (the Ursine call it Den-oak).

> **DRAFT.** Everything under "Traits" and "Names" is a proposal written by the assistant, not established lore. The owner will revisit it in session 0.

## Traits (draft)

- **Size.** Medium. Base walking speed 30 feet.
- **Claws.** Your claws are a natural weapon. Your unarmed strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage.
- **Winter Coat.** You have resistance to cold damage.
- **Keen Nose.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Lean Season.** As a bonus action, you gain temporary hit points equal to your level. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses after a long rest.
- **Languages.** Common and Ursine (not recorded).

# Ursine

Bipedal bear-folk, native to [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md), where they live in relatively high numbers. Playable. Their two major cities are [Frostden](../continents/berksheland/cities/frostden/README.md) in the icy northwest and [Honeyhearth](../continents/berksheland/cities/honeyhearth/README.md) in the comfortable southwest. There is tension between the Ursine and the [Castori](castori.md), who are expanding their digging of [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) (the Ursine call it Den-oak).

> **DRAFT.** Everything under "Traits" and "Names" is a proposal written by the assistant, not established lore. The owner will revisit it in session 0.

## Name ridges in Den-oak

Some Den-oak grows with ridges that spell Ursine names in an ancient Ursine language. A piece whose name matches your own is very lucky, and expecting mothers see one as a sign. The language is ancient, so only a few Ursine know what to look for and what the ridges mean; the [Den-Keepers](../factions/den-keepers.md) preserve it through their elders and [The Tonguestone](../continents/berksheland/locations/the-tonguestone.md), a monolith in the icy north. See [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md).

## Traits (draft)

- **Size.** Medium. Base walking speed 30 feet.
- **Claws.** Your claws are a natural weapon. Your unarmed strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage.
- **Winter Coat.** You have resistance to cold damage.
- **Keen Nose.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
- **Lean Season.** As a bonus action, you gain temporary hit points equal to your level. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses after a long rest.
- **Languages.** Common and Ursine (not recorded).

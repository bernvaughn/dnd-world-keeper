# Castori (Beaverkin)

A Sead custom species. The rivers of Sead belong to the Beaverkin. Stocky, broad-tailed, and endlessly industrious, the Castori (Beaverkin, to everyone else) raise dam-towns across [Faircliff](../continents/sead/locations/faircliff.md)'s waterways: whole villages of timber and mud that rise in a single season and are rebuilt, bigger, the next. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

A Beaverkin lodge is family, fortress, and business all at once: nobody works alone, nobody eats alone, and nobody threatens the dam. Beaverkin teeth grow their whole lives, so a Castori is always chewing something. They regard a well-built structure the way other folk regard a well-told story, and they judge strangers by their handshake, their work ethic, and whether they offer to help carry the logs. Castori work on the [Ardmont](../continents/sead/cities/ardmont/README.md) docks as stevedores and factory hands.

## Traits

- **Age.** Beaverkin are grown at 8, considered elders at 45, and rarely live past 60.
- **Alignment.** Beaverkin lean lawful and good, in that order of importance. The lodge comes first; decency comes naturally to anyone raised on shared work and shared meals. A Castori who abandons their lodge is a rare and pitied thing.
- **Size.** Beaverkin stand 3 to 4 feet tall, broad and heavily built for their height, with powerful flat tails nearly as long as their legs. They weigh 50 to 90 pounds. Your size is Small.
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 25 feet. You have a swimming speed of 30 feet.
- **Hold Breath.** You can hold your breath for up to 15 minutes at a time.
- **Felling Bite.** Your ever-growing teeth are a natural weapon. Your bite is an unarmed strike that deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier piercing damage. Your bite deals double damage to objects and structures made of wood.
- **Master Builder.** You have proficiency with carpenter's tools. In addition, you can spend 10 minutes reinforcing a wooden door, gate, barricade, or similar structure within reach, doubling its hit points. A structure can benefit from this only once until it is repaired.
- **Cold-Weather Pelt.** Your dense, waterproof double coat gives you resistance to cold damage.
- **Warning Slap.** While you are in water, you can use a bonus action to slap the surface with your broad tail. Every ally within 60 feet of you who can hear the slap is alerted to danger: alerted allies are not surprised, and sleeping allies wake.
- **Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Castori. Castori is a rapid, chittering language full of tooth-clicks and tail-thumps; written Castori uses neat, diagram-like glyphs adapted from construction plans.

## Names

Beaverkin take a given name at birth and carry their lodge's name for life. Leaving a lodge to found a new one is the proudest moment of a Castori's life, and the only time they take a new name.

- **Given names:** Alder, Aspen, Birch, Brook, Cedar, Hazel, Maple, Otter, Reed, Russet, Tamarack, Willow
- **Lodge names:** Deeproot, Highdam, Smoothstone, Stillwater, Talltimber, Widewater

## Castori in Berksheland

Castori are expanding their operations in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md), digging up [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md), an ancient petrified wood under the ice that is especially useful for machine construction. [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) runs the bulk of these operations. The [Ursine](ursine.md), native to Berksheland, are in tension with the Castori over it. The Castori treat the wood as simply excellent, and think the Ursine's claims about its name ridges are made up to be hostile.

## Castori in Sead

- **Homeland:** Beaverkin are concentrated in Ardmont but can be found on any river in Sead: wherever there is running water, there is likely a lodge. Dam-towns sell timber, engineering, and flood control to their neighbors.
- **Adventurers:** A Castori leaves the lodge to see how the rest of the world builds, and usually comes home with strong opinions. Young Beaverkin especially feel the pull: there is always a river they have not dammed yet.

# Yeti

A variant of the Goliath species (Player's Handbook, 2024), similar to Goliaths but with more cold resistance. Uncommon but present in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md). Playable.

> **DRAFT.** Everything under "Traits" is a proposal written by the assistant, not established lore. The owner will revisit it in session 0.

## Traits (draft)

Use the Goliath traits from the Player's Handbook (2024), with these changes:

- **Frost Ancestry.** Replaces Giant Ancestry. You have resistance to cold damage, and you automatically pass saving throws and ability checks made to resist the effects of extreme cold.
- **Snowbound.** You ignore difficult terrain caused by snow and ice.
- **Frost's Chill.** As Frost's Chill in the Goliath's Giant Ancestry, but the extra cold damage is 1d8 and the speed reduction applies to creatures that fail a Constitution save instead of automatically (save DC not recorded).

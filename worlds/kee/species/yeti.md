# Yeti

A variant of the Goliath species (Player's Handbook, 2024), similar to Goliaths but with more cold resistance. Uncommon but present in [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md). Playable. Their town is [Farkight](../continents/berksheland/cities/farkight/README.md), in the northeast.

Traits confirmed by the owner in session 0 (2026-11-24).

## Traits

- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (about 7-8 feet tall)
- **Speed:** 35 feet

**Frost Ancestry.** Replaces the Goliath's Giant Ancestry choice. You have resistance to cold damage, and you automatically succeed on saving throws and ability checks made to resist the effects of extreme cold.

**Frost's Chill.** Same as Frost's Chill in the Goliath's Giant Ancestry. When you hit a target with an attack roll and deal damage to it, you can also deal 1d6 cold damage to the target and reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. You can use this a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

**Snowbound.** You ignore difficult terrain caused by snow and ice.

**Large Form.** As the Goliath trait. Starting at character level 5, you can change your size to Large as a Bonus Action if you are in a big enough space. It lasts 10 minutes or until you end it (no action required). For that duration you have Advantage on Strength checks and your Speed increases by 10 feet. Once used, it can't be used again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Powerful Build.** As the Goliath trait. You have Advantage on any ability check you make to end the Grappled condition, and you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity.

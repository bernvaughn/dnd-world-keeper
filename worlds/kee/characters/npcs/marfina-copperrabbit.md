# Marfina Copperrabbit

A halfling of Khenmeld, fatally injured by Mabub in the first round of the Tournament of Champions. From Discord messages. Remembered for her gentle nature and her love of shiny pebbles. Her surname matches one of the [Applewood families](../../factions/applewood-families.md); the post does not say whether she belonged to it.

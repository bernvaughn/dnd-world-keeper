An [Ursine](../../species/ursine.md) widow who owns [The Sleeping Anchor](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/the-sleeping-anchor.md), an inn in Coldanchor.

> **PARTLY PROPOSED.** Dagny was suggested by the assistant and approved by the owner, who confirmed she is a widow. Details under "Proposed" are not confirmed.

Dagny has not noticed a missing fur blanket, and the owner expects her to assume she misplaced it.

# Vorru-Kesh Blackreed

High Sentinel of [Murkwallow](../../continents/sead/cities/murkwallow/README.md), overall commander of the [Gilded Reed Sentinels](../../factions/gilded-reed-sentinels.md), and ruler in practice of [Cliffspire](../../continents/sead/locations/cliffspire.md). Species and pronouns are not recorded; the Sentinels' officers are mainly bullywugs and grung. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

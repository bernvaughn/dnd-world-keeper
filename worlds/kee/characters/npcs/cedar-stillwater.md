# Cedar Stillwater

A [Castori](../species/castori.md) elder of Stillwater lodge, who set [Reed Stillwater](reed-stillwater.md) up as the inexperienced driver of the shipment in the escort hook at [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md). He assumed no harm would come to Reed. Whether he is right is left to play.

## Proposed, not confirmed

- Around 52 years old, soft-spoken and stubborn.
- Believes the Ursine are hurting too, and that nobody dies over wood.
- Did not want Reed hurt, but did want the haul to fail, since the Stillwater lodge is worn out by [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) quotas.
- He might turn up at the wharf after the ambush and face Reed.

## Not recorded

- Whether the rest of the lodge knows.
- His relationship to the Den-Keepers, if any, and whether he knew the Ursine would be the ones to ambush.

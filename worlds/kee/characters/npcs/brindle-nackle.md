# Captain Brindle "Boomfizz" Nackle

A gnome pirate/mercenary of [Saltmaril](../../continents/sead/cities/saltmaril/README.md) (he), and adoptive parent of [Orla](../pcs/orla.md) together with [Tikka Wrenchgut](tikka-wrenchgut.md); they were also her old crew. From the Book of Sead 2.0 and the owner.

Captain of the [Sizzlewhip](../../ships/sizzlewhip.md). Was desperate for help recovering the [Obsidian Eye](../../items/obsidian-eye.md), and accepted it from the party, then revealed that the Sizzlewhip could fly the entire time (From Discord messages. See [session-2025-07-22-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-07-22-dm2.md)).

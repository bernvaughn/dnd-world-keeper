# Reed Stillwater

A [Castori](../species/castori.md) driver of the Bruinwood shipment in the escort hook at [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md). He is inexperienced, and has been set up as the driver to make the Den-Keepers' ambush more likely to succeed. Players may use Insight (DC 12) to discover this. Stillwater is one of the Castori lodge names.

## Set-up

Reed's lodge elder, [Cedar Stillwater](cedar-stillwater.md), set him up as the driver, assuming no harm would come to him. Whether the elder is right is left to play.

# Mythanar Sardan

Archdruid of [Myrddin](../../continents/sead/locations/myrddin.md): a Wood Elf of the Circle of the Moon (he). Leads the [Circle of the Moon](../../factions/circle-of-the-moon.md) and effectively runs Myrddin. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

Asked Mabub to check on Greenwood, and later gave his blessing to reviving it, along with four draft horses and the aid of [Aerin](aerin.md) (From Discord messages. See [session-2025-06-24-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-06-24-dm2.md) and [session-2025-07-01-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-07-01-dm2.md).)

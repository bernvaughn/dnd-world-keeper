# Hesper Voss

An [Ursine](../../species/ursine.md) woman, a dockhand and regular at [The Thawed Berth](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/the-thawed-berth.md). She resents the [Bruinwood](../../items/bruinwood.md) shipments but never says so near Castori.

## The leak

She is the one who leaked the shipment's route to the [Den-Keepers](../factions/den-keepers.md), by telling [Grask Ironmaw](grask-ironmaw.md). She regrets letting it slip, because she knows he is likely to try something. The players are not expected to know this.

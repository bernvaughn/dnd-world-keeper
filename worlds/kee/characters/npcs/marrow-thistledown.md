# Marrow Thistledown

An older [Ursine](../../species/ursine.md) woman, owner of [The Thawed Berth](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/the-thawed-berth.md) in Coldanchor. She keeps a pot of honey-spiced stew on all day and hears every rumor, sharing them for the price of a drink. She keeps the frozen-anchor legend alive by pointing to a rusted anchor over the hearth.

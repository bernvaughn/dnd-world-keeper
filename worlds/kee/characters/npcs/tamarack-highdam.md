# Tamarack Highdam

A [Castori](../species/castori.md) foreman who runs [The Cradle Yard](../continents/berksheland/cities/longdam/locations/the-cradle-yard.md) in Longdam. He thinks the name-ridged pieces of [Den-oak](../items/bruinwood.md) are neat, but has no idea how important they are to the [Den-Keepers](../factions/den-keepers.md). Highdam is one of the Castori lodge names.

## Proposed, not confirmed

- Friendly, and fascinated by the ridges as pretty wood.

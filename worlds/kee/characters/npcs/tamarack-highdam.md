# Tamarack Highdam

A [Castori](../species/castori.md) foreman who runs [The Cradle Yard](../continents/berksheland/cities/longdam/locations/the-cradle-yard.md) in Longdam. He thinks the name-ridged pieces of [Den-oak](../items/bruinwood.md) are neat, but has no idea how important they are to the [Den-Keepers](../factions/den-keepers.md). Highdam is one of the Castori lodge names. He is the cousin of [Alder Highdam](alder-highdam.md), the dockmaster at the other end of the shipment's route, and a member of Highdam lodge, which is thriving off Gnawfast.

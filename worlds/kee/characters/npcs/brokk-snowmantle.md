# Brokk Snowmantle

A [Yeti](../species/yeti.md), the leader of [Hearthward](../continents/berksheland/locations/hearthward.md), the small settlement near [The Tonguestone](../continents/berksheland/locations/the-tonguestone.md). He likes the [Den-Keepers](../factions/den-keepers.md): they bring supplies to his town often and are very respectful of the area. He knows the Tonguestone is very important to them.

## Proposed, not confirmed

- A broad, steady Yeti who has housed pilgrims for decades and is respected by Ursine and Yeti alike.

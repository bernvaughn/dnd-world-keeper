# Brokk Snowmantle

A [Yeti](../species/yeti.md), the leader of [Hearthward](../continents/berksheland/locations/hearthward.md), the small settlement near [The Tonguestone](../continents/berksheland/locations/the-tonguestone.md). He likes the [Den-Keepers](../factions/den-keepers.md): they bring supplies to his town often and are very respectful of the area. He knows the Tonguestone is very important to them. He has no strong personal feelings about the [Castori](../species/castori.md) digging, but he knows it upsets his Den-Keeper friends, so he is against it.

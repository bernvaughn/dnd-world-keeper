A gnome pirate/mercenary of [Saltmaril](../../continents/sead/cities/saltmaril/README.md), and adoptive parent of [Orla](../pcs/orla.md) together with [Captain Brindle "Boomfizz" Nackle](brindle-nackle.md). Pronouns are not recorded. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

Quoted the cost of fitting the [Maboat](../../ships/maboat.md) with elemental engines: 15,000 gp each, 60,000 gp total (From Discord messages. See [session-2025-07-22-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-07-22-dm2.md)).

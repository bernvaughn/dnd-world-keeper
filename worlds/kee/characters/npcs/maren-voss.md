# Maren Voss

An [Ursine](../species/ursine.md), the late wife of [Grask Ironmaw](grask-ironmaw.md) and the sister of [Hesper Voss](hesper-voss.md). She was a hired laborer who died in a tunnel collapse at a [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) dig. The company paid a small settlement and blamed her for the collapse. Her death is the root of Grask's grievance and, through him, of the Den-Keepers' ambush in the escort hook at [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md).

## Her name in the Den-oak

One piece of naturally name-ridged [Den-oak](../items/bruinwood.md) in the shipment bears Maren Voss's name, and there are probably several "Voss" ones. This makes the ambush personal for Grask and Hesper.

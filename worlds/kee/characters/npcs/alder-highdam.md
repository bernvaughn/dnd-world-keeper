# Alder Highdam

A [Castori](../../species/castori.md) dockmaster at [Bruinwood Wharf](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md) in Coldanchor, who runs the loading crews. He is brisk and proud of his work, and is employed by [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md). Highdam is one of the Castori lodge names. He is the cousin of [Tamarack Highdam](tamarack-highdam.md), the foreman at the Cradle Yard, and a member of Highdam lodge, which is thriving off Gnawfast. Like most Castori, he thinks the Ursine's claims about the name ridges are made up. He does not know the name-ridged pieces are in the shipment, and does not know to care about them: they are not officially recorded, just a novelty workers may notice while gathering and loading the shipment, without thinking much about it.

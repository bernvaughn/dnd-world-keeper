# Vercani Vuldici

King of Sead. Eldest son of [King Victor Vuldici](victor-vuldici.md), and twin brother of [Prince Viu Vuldici](viu-vuldici.md) (he). From Discord messages.

Inherited the throne when his father died, and as crown prince hosted the Tournament of Champions to bring the kingdom together. Gave the speech at the feast in [session-2025-06-03-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-06-03-dm2.md). Was officially crowned King of Sead at the end of the tournament ([session-2025-06-17-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-06-17-dm2.md)).

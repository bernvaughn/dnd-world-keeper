# Dorian Vale

A human steward on the Bruinwood shipment in the escort hook at [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md). He is cagey about the name-ridged pieces mixed in with the [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md), which are not officially recorded. He knows the pieces are important to the Ursine, but not exactly why. He knows the shipment has to be delivered to meet quota, and that he earns a bonus for it. He will be worse off if the ambush succeeds.

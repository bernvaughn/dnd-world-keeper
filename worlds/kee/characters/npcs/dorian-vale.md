# Dorian Vale

A human steward on the Bruinwood shipment in the escort hook at [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md). He is cagey about the heirlooms mixed in with the [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md). He knows the shipment has to be delivered to meet quota, and that he earns a bonus for it. He will be worse off if the ambush succeeds.

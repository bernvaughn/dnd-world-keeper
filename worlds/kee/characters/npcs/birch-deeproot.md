# Birch Deeproot

A [Castori](../../species/castori.md) foreman off a [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) ship, and a regular at [The Thawed Berth](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/the-thawed-berth.md). He is tired and pragmatic, complaining about quotas but defending the work. Deeproot is one of the Castori lodge names.

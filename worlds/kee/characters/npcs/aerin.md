# Aerin

A young elf druid (he) who can wildshape. From Discord messages.

Mythanar gifted Mabub's Greenwood venture the aid of a young wildling named Aerin. At Greenwood he used the remains of the town and the fire to grow an apple tree in remembrance. See [session-2025-07-01-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-07-01-dm2.md).

A halfling rogue (she). Player character. Created in session 0 (2026-11-24).

## Notes

- Arrives in [Coldanchor](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md), [Berksheland](../../continents/berksheland/README.md), on [The Stubborn Tern](../../ships/the-stubborn-tern.md), travelling with [Ryu Boku](ryu-boku.md).
- On the run from [The Gilded Pantry](../../factions/gilded-pantry.md), a halfling crime syndicate. She was not associated with them: she stole a beautiful set of leather armor from the personal stash of the matron, [Auntie Plumsworth](../npcs/auntie-plumsworth.md), without realizing who they were.

# Veralyn Fastfeet

A halfling rogue (she). Player character. Created in session 0 (2026-11-24).

## Notes

- Arrives in [Coldanchor](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md), [Berksheland](../../continents/berksheland/README.md), on [The Stubborn Tern](../../ships/the-stubborn-tern.md), travelling with [Ryu Boku](ryu-boku.md).
- On the run from [The Gilded Pantry](../../factions/gilded-pantry.md), a halfling crime syndicate. She was not associated with them: she stole a beautiful set of leather armor ([Plumsworth's Hushstitch Leathers](../../items/hushstitch-leathers.md), a Common magic item) from the personal stash of the matron, [Auntie Plumsworth](../npcs/auntie-plumsworth.md), without realizing who they were.
- In [session 2026-11-24](../../sessions/session-2026-11-24-dm1.md): stole food from [Dozy Dumplings](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/dozy-dumplings.md) unnoticed, met [Cornelius Tundrafluff](cornelius-tundrafluff.md), and got a free room at [The Sleeping Anchor](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/the-sleeping-anchor.md) by lying to [Dagny Thornfeld](../npcs/dagny-thornfeld.md) that she was on [Alder Highdam](../npcs/alder-highdam.md)'s tab. She stole a fur blanket there, which Dagny has not noticed.

# Ryu Boku

A [Castori](../../species/castori.md) fighter (he). Player character. Created in session 0 (2026-11-24). Also written "Ryuboku" (one word); both spellings are in use, so watch for which one sticks.

## Notes

- Homeland is [Xianhua](../../continents/xianhua/README.md).
- Named after the driftwood he was found in as a kit. He was rescued by the Floodedfield den and adopted, and raised in their den as one of their own.
- He trained as a samurai in secret. When he was discovered, he was deemed ronin, stripped of his den name, and exiled to the frozen north.
- Has no den name now (the species file calls it a lodge name). If he survives the cold and proves himself worthy, he will be allowed to join the Castori Clan formally and be given his den name back.
- He knows nothing about his birth family, what happened to them, or how he ended up in the driftwood that morning.
- Travels with [Veralyn Fastfeet](veralyn-fastfeet.md) on [The Stubborn Tern](../../ships/the-stubborn-tern.md) to [Coldanchor](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md) in [Berksheland](../../continents/berksheland/README.md).

# Cornelius Tundrafluff

A [Yeti](../../species/yeti.md) artificer (he/him). Player character. Created in session 0 (2026-11-24).

## Notes

- From [Hearthward](../../continents/berksheland/locations/hearthward.md), the Yeti-run town in [Berksheland](../../continents/berksheland/README.md).
- Runs a snow cone stall at the market in [Coldanchor](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md).
- Went to school in Berksheland, at [Kettlefrost College](../../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/kettlefrost-college.md) in Coldanchor.
- His mother, [Sherpa Tundrafluff](../npcs/sherpa-tundrafluff.md), moved to Coldanchor to be closer to him while he was in school and after. He lives with her now.

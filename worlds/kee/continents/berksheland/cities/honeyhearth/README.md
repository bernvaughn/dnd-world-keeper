# Honeyhearth

One of the two major [Ursine](../../../../species/ursine.md) cities in [Berksheland](../../README.md). It lies in the comfortable south, in the southwest of the continent. Because it is easier for creatures without natural cold resistance to live here, it has a more diverse mix of peoples than [Frostden](../frostden/README.md). It has a large honey-related bear industry.

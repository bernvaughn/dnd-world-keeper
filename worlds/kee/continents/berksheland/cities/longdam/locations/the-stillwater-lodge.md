# The Stillwater Lodge

A [Castori](../../../../../species/castori.md) lodge-house in [Longdam](../README.md), home to Stillwater lodge, the lodge of [Reed Stillwater](../../../../../characters/npcs/reed-stillwater.md) and [Cedar Stillwater](../../../../../characters/npcs/cedar-stillwater.md).

## Contrast with Highdam lodge

Stillwater lodge is worn out by [Gnawfast](../../../../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) quotas, while Highdam lodge, the lodge of [Tamarack](../../../../../characters/npcs/tamarack-highdam.md) and [Alder Highdam](../../../../../characters/npcs/alder-highdam.md), is thriving off the same company. This helps explain why [Cedar](../../../../../characters/npcs/cedar-stillwater.md) set Reed up.

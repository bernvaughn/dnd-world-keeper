# Longdam

The company town of [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md), where the bulk of the [Castori](../../../../species/castori.md) operations in [Berksheland](../../README.md) are run. It sits in the middle of the continent, on the split between the icy north and the comfortable south, and stretches east to west as a long town. Homes are on the comfortable southern side, with easy access to the icy north, where the [Bruinwood](../../../../items/bruinwood.md) is dug. The escort shipment in the [Bruinwood Wharf](../coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md) hook starts here, so the players can explore a little first.

## Demographics

A majority Castori and Dwarf population, with a healthy mix of other races. It is a relatively nice place to live, with plenty of work available.

## Layout (north to south)

- **The Cut:** the northern edge, where tunnels and haul-roads run into the ice. It is the working side, loud and cold.
- **The Long Row:** the main east-west street, with the company offices, depots, and shops.
- **Southside:** the homes, gardens, and lodges on the comfortable side.

## Locations

- [Gnawfast Hall](locations/gnawfast-hall.md): the company headquarters, where quotas are posted.
- [The Stillwater Lodge](locations/the-stillwater-lodge.md): a Castori lodge-house.
- [The Gnawing Post](locations/the-gnawing-post.md): a tavern where off-shift crews drink.
- [The Cradle Yard](locations/the-cradle-yard.md): where Bruinwood is cut, sorted, and numbered into cradles.
- [Thawline Clinic](locations/thawline-clinic.md): a small infirmary that handles dig injuries.

# Longdam

The company town of [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md), where the bulk of the [Castori](../../../../species/castori.md) operations in [Berksheland](../../README.md) are run. It sits in the middle of the continent, on the split between the icy north and the comfortable south, and stretches east to west as a long town. Homes are on the comfortable southern side, with easy access to the icy north, where the [Bruinwood](../../../../items/bruinwood.md) is dug.

# Kettlefrost College

A college in [Coldanchor](../README.md) that teaches culinary arts and arcane study together, on the idea that cooking is wizardry done slowly. [Cornelius Tundrafluff](../../../../../characters/pcs/cornelius-tundrafluff.md) studied here.

> **PROPOSAL.** The college, its name, and its courses were proposed by the assistant in session 0 and the owner liked the concept. Only its location in Coldanchor and Cornelius attending are stated by the owner. The rest is not established lore.

## Courses (proposed)

- **Cold-Kitchen Magic:** preserving food with frost and keeping hearths lit in blizzards.
- **Alchemical Gastronomy:** brewing, mead, and honey-based potions.
- **Thermal Evocation:** controlling heat and cold, such as flash-freezing, searing, and shaving ice.
- **Provisioning:** feeding crews and caravans.

## Alumni

- [Cornelius Tundrafluff](../../../../../characters/pcs/cornelius-tundrafluff.md): a Yeti artificer from Hearthward who runs a snow cone stall at the market.

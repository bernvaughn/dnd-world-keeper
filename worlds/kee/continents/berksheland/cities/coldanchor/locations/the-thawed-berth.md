# The Thawed Berth

A tavern near the docks of [Coldanchor](../README.md), where crews swap news. A likely place for the game to start. A rusted anchor over the hearth is said to recall the legend of the frozen cold anchor.

## Characters

- [Marrow Thistledown](../../../../../characters/npcs/marrow-thistledown.md): the Ursine owner, who keeps a pot of honey-spiced stew on all day and trades rumors for drinks.
- [Birch Deeproot](../../../../../characters/npcs/birch-deeproot.md): a tired, pragmatic Castori foreman off a Gnawfast ship.
- [Old Torvik](../../../../../characters/npcs/old-torvik.md): a Yeti former sailor who drinks mead and talks little.
- [Pell Quickwater](../../../../../characters/npcs/pell-quickwater.md): a halfling fabric trader newly in from Sead.
- [Hesper Voss](../../../../../characters/npcs/hesper-voss.md): an Ursine woman and dockhand who resents the Bruinwood shipments.

- [Kessik Sunscale](../../../../../characters/npcs/kessik-sunscale.md): a lizardfolk with his chair awkwardly close to the hearth, if the players haven't already met him. He asks them to help him stay warm and to find the Bruinwood.

## Rumors

Whether each is true is not recorded.

1. The wharf crews are digging closer to something the Ursine consider sacred, and Den-oak is more than just wood to them.
2. [Lord Butterclaw](../../../../../characters/npcs/lord-butterclaw.md) is quietly refusing a bigger Gnawfast offer for exclusive export rights.
3. A ship came in with fruit that was frozen solid and not quite what it claimed to be.
4. The cold anchor wasn't the first thing the original sailors left in the ice.

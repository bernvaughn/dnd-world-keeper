# Bruinwood Wharf

The wharf in [Coldanchor](../README.md) where [Bruinwood](../../../../../items/bruinwood.md) is shipped out. The [Castori](../../../../../species/castori.md) export a huge amount of it, so the tension with the [Ursine](../../../../../species/ursine.md) over the wood is visible here.

## What you see

- Stacks of petrified Bruinwood in numbered cradles, each ready to be hauled onto a ship.
- A cold-forged crane built with Bruinwood parts, the Castori's showpiece.
- Ursine protesters on the pier now and then, near a spot where Lord Butterclaw's guards keep the peace.

## Characters

- [Alder Highdam](../../../../../characters/npcs/alder-highdam.md): the Castori dockmaster, a Gnawfast employee who runs the loading crews.
- [Kessa Greywhisker](../../../../../characters/npcs/kessa-greywhisker.md): an Ursine tally-keeper whose count both sides trust.
- [Brindle](../../../../../characters/npcs/brindle.md): a half-grown Castori runner who knows every shortcut.

## Adventure hook: the escort (proposed, DM spoilers)

Not yet played. This is a proposal agreed with the owner.

1. Alder Highdam needs a big Bruinwood shipment moved from [Longdam](../../longdam/README.md) to a ship at the wharf. Recent "accidents" mean he can't trust his own crews, so he hires the players, who have no side in the Castori-Ursine tension, and tells them to avoid the main road. The shipment starts in Longdam, so the players can explore a little first.
2. Someone has told the [Den-Keepers](../../../../../factions/den-keepers.md) the route. The leak did not come from the shipment crew, who are all worse off if the ambush succeeds. It came from [Hesper Voss](../../../../../characters/npcs/hesper-voss.md), the Ursine dockhand.
3. The Den-Keepers ambush the shipment at the Ice Cut, a narrow channel through frozen rock where the cart can't turn around. They try to intimidate the players into handing it over. About half of the Den-oak in the shipment is actually carved heirlooms that are lost Ursine cultural treasures, and they want those specific pieces.
4. They usually don't need to fight. [Grask Ironmaw](../../../../../characters/npcs/grask-ironmaw.md) is a real threat if combat starts, and their leader, [Orsa Quillmane](../../../../../characters/npcs/orsa-quillmane.md), will negotiate if the players resist.

## The shipment crew

- [Reed Stillwater](../../../../../characters/npcs/reed-stillwater.md): a Castori driver, inexperienced and set up to make the ambush more likely to succeed by his own lodge elder, who assumed no harm would come to him. Players may use Insight (DC 12) to discover this.
- [Hazel Talltimber](../../../../../characters/npcs/hazel-talltimber.md): a Castori second hand.
- [Dorian Vale](../../../../../characters/npcs/dorian-vale.md): a human steward, cagey about the heirlooms. He needs the shipment delivered to meet quota and earn his bonus.

# Bruinwood Wharf

The wharf in [Coldanchor](../README.md) where [Bruinwood](../../../../../items/bruinwood.md) is shipped out. The [Castori](../../../../../species/castori.md) export a huge amount of it, so the tension with the [Ursine](../../../../../species/ursine.md) over the wood is visible here.

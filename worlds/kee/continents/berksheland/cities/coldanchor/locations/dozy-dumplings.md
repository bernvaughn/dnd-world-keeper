# Dozy Dumplings

A food stall at the edge of the market in [Coldanchor](../README.md), near the docks and a couple of stalls down from [Cornelius Tundrafluff](../../../../../characters/pcs/cornelius-tundrafluff.md)'s snow cone cart. [Veralyn Fastfeet](../../../../../characters/pcs/veralyn-fastfeet.md) stole food from it without being noticed (see [session-2026-11-24-dm1](../../../../../sessions/session-2026-11-24-dm1.md)).

> **PARTLY PROPOSED.** The owner approved the stall and its name. Everything under "Proposed" was suggested by the assistant and has not been confirmed.

## Proposed (not confirmed)

- A honey-bun stall: honey-glazed buns kept warm on low open racks, smoked fish dumplings, and mead-stewed apples.
- Run by [Tobble Hearthpaw](../../../../../characters/npcs/tobble-hearthpaw.md), a very old, nearly deaf Ursine who naps on a stool beside the steamer.

## Characters

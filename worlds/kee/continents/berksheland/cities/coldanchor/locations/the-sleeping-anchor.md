# The Sleeping Anchor

An inn in [Coldanchor](../README.md). [Alder Highdam](../../../../../characters/npcs/alder-highdam.md) put [Ryu Boku](../../../../../characters/pcs/ryu-boku.md) and [Kessik Sunscale](../../../../../characters/npcs/kessik-sunscale.md) up here for the night after they took the Bruinwood escort job (see the [Bruinwood Wharf](bruinwood-wharf.md) hook).

> **PARTLY PROPOSED.** The owner confirmed the name, the Coldanchor location, and that Alder booked the night's rooms here. Everything under "Proposed" was suggested by the assistant and has not been confirmed.

## Established

- The name is The Sleeping Anchor.
- It is an inn in Coldanchor.
- Alder Highdam arranged lodging here for the night for Ryu Boku and Kessik Sunscale.

## Proposed (not confirmed)

- A long, low building made from salvaged ship timbers, a short walk from [Bruinwood Wharf](bruinwood-wharf.md).
- A frozen anchor is carved into the lintel, a nod to the town's legend. Guests joke about not getting "stuck" for the night.
- The hearths are kept lit all night, and the big hearth in the common room is a warm spot for guests who feel the cold.
- [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../../../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) keeps a standing account here, so Alder's guests get rooms without questions.
- A shared bunkroom for sailors, plus a few private rooms.

## Characters

- [Dagny Thornfeld](../../../../../characters/npcs/dagny-thornfeld.md): the Ursine owner (proposed).

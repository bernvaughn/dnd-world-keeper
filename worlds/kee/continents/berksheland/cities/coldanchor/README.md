# Coldanchor

A large, diversely mixed port town in the south of [Berksheland](../../README.md). It is independent, and a powerful influence over the region itself, with a lot of [Ursine](../../../../species/ursine.md) influence, since they are native to the continent. It is run by [Lord Butterclaw](../../../../characters/npcs/lord-butterclaw.md), who gets along well with beavers and bears alike. A lot of trade comes in and out of it to and from other continents.

## Name

People say the sorry sailors who discovered the land let down their cold anchor, and it froze to the ground and left them stuck. Whether this is true is not recorded.

## Trade

- **Imports:** a lot of supplies, especially fabrics, fruits, and vegetables that don't grow well on the continent.
- **Exports:** [Bruinwood](../../../../items/bruinwood.md), the petrified wood, of which the [Castori](../../../../species/castori.md) export a huge amount, and honey-related products such as mead and honey itself.

## Layout and life

Big docks, a big market, and everything else that makes up a sizeable city. Much of the city is built around sailors and trade. Sailors buy drinks and trinkets native to the continent, and natives come here to buy supplies to distribute throughout the continent.

## Tension

The Castori and Ursine generally get along here, but some tension exists because the Castori export so much of the native petrified wood.

## Locations

- [The Thawed Berth](locations/the-thawed-berth.md): a tavern near the docks where crews swap news.
- [Bruinwood Wharf](locations/bruinwood-wharf.md): where Bruinwood is shipped out.
- [The Harbormaster's Ledger](locations/the-harbormasters-ledger.md): the port office.
- [Honeycask Exchange](locations/honeycask-exchange.md): a trading hall for honey products.
- [The Rimed Compass](locations/the-rimed-compass.md): a shop for maps, charts, and cold-weather gear.

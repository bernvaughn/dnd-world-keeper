# Hearthward

A small active settlement near [The Tonguestone](the-tonguestone.md) in the icy north of [Berksheland](../README.md), populated mostly by [Yetis](../../../species/yeti.md) and [Ursine](../../../species/ursine.md). Pilgrims, such as the [Den-Keepers](../../../factions/den-keepers.md), stay here on their journey to the stone, if they do not camp out in the icy wasteland. It is run by a Yeti.

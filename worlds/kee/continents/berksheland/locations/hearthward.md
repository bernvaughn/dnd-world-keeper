# Hearthward

A small active settlement near [The Tonguestone](the-tonguestone.md) in the icy north of [Berksheland](../README.md), populated mostly by [Yetis](../../../species/yeti.md) and [Ursine](../../../species/ursine.md). Pilgrims, such as the [Den-Keepers](../../../factions/den-keepers.md), stay here on their journey to the stone, if they do not camp out in the icy wasteland. It is run by [Brokk Snowmantle](../../../characters/npcs/brokk-snowmantle.md), a Yeti, who likes the Den-Keepers: they bring supplies often and are very respectful of the area.

## People

Many of the Ursine in town are familiar with the Tonguestone and know it is of cultural significance, but people who stay in town tend to be less zealous than the Den-Keepers. They are also separated from the gathering and exporting of the wood.

# The Tonguestone

A large Rosetta-stone-style monolith in the icy north of [Berksheland](../README.md), which holds the ancient Ursine language of the name ridges in [Den-oak](../../../items/bruinwood.md). It is safe from most people because the region is cold and treacherous. The [Den-Keepers](../../../factions/den-keepers.md) make pilgrimages to it to observe the stone and preserve the language. It is not linked to the buried Ursine settlement ruins where the name-ridged Den-oak in the Bruinwood Wharf shipment was found.

## Pilgrims

A small active settlement nearby, populated mostly by [Yetis](../../../species/yeti.md) and [Ursine](../../../species/ursine.md), is where pilgrims stay on their journey, if they do not camp out in the icy wasteland. Its name is not recorded.

# Berksheland

A cold continent far north of [Sead](../sead/README.md) and [Xianhua](../xianhua/README.md), with a climate similar to Greenland. It has been settled by other peoples, so the population is a standard mix, but it is the native homeland of the [Ursine](../../species/ursine.md), bipedal bear-folk, who live here in relatively high numbers. [Yetis](../../species/yeti.md) are uncommon but present. Created for a new game in the kee world.

## Bruinwood

An ancient petrified wood runs deep under Berksheland's ice. It is especially useful for machine construction. The [Castori](../../species/castori.md) call it [Bruinwood](../../items/bruinwood.md); the Ursine call it Den-oak.

## Tension: Ursine and Castori

The Castori are expanding their operations in Berksheland, digging up the Bruinwood, and the Ursine are in tension with them over it. The bulk of the Castori operations run under [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md).

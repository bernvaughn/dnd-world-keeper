# Berksheland

A cold continent far north of [Sead](../sead/README.md) and [Xianhua](../xianhua/README.md), with a climate similar to Greenland. It has been settled by other peoples, so the population is a standard mix, but it is the native homeland of the [Ursine](../../species/ursine.md), bipedal bear-folk, who live here in relatively high numbers. [Yetis](../../species/yeti.md) are uncommon but present. Created for a new game in the kee world.

## Geography

A lumpy circle with a split down the middle.

- **The north** is an icy wasteland.
- **The south** is still cold, but mostly comfortable for races that don't mind harsh winters and chilly days.

## Cities

- [Longdam](cities/longdam/README.md): the [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) company town, a long east-west town in the middle of the continent, with homes on the comfortable side and easy access to the icy north.
- [Frostden](cities/frostden/README.md): a major Ursine city in the icy northwest.
- [Honeyhearth](cities/honeyhearth/README.md): a major Ursine city in the comfortable southwest, more diverse than Frostden, with a large honey-related bear industry.
- [Farkight](cities/farkight/README.md): the Yeti town, in the northeast.
- [Coldanchor](cities/coldanchor/README.md): a large, diversely mixed, independent port town in the south, run by [Lord Butterclaw](../../characters/npcs/lord-butterclaw.md), where a lot of trade comes in and out to and from other continents.

## Bruinwood

An ancient petrified wood runs deep under Berksheland's ice. It is especially useful for machine construction. The [Castori](../../species/castori.md) call it [Bruinwood](../../items/bruinwood.md); the Ursine call it Den-oak. Some of it grows with ridges that spell Ursine names, which makes it sacred and lucky to them, and the Castori are finding more pockets of it.

## Tension: Ursine and Castori

The Castori are expanding their operations in Berksheland, digging up the Bruinwood, and the Ursine are in tension with them over it. The bulk of the Castori operations run under Gnawfast Excavation Co., out of Longdam.

## Locations

- [The Tonguestone](locations/the-tonguestone.md): a large Rosetta-stone-style monolith in the icy north, where the [Den-Keepers](../../factions/den-keepers.md) make pilgrimages to preserve the ancient Ursine language.

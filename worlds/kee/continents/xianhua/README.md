# Xianhua

Another continent, southeast of [Sead](../sead/README.md), with an oriental theme. It is known almost entirely from its map; little else is recorded of it. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## Regions and places labeled on the map

- **Huanyin Isles**: islands to the west: Zhuyin, Luo'ao
- **Shanyu Rainlands**: north-center: Qingyun, Qhiuling
- **Jinun**: the mountainous northeast: Takuan
- **Shntan Forest**: west: Tanwei, Heiyuan (spelled "Shntan" on the map)
- **Wanhai Delta**: center-south: Ximeng
- **Zhentan Forest**: south
- **Guuaing**: an island in the southern bay
- **Linghai Coast**: the eastern and southern coast: Bailu, Haice

## Cities

None recorded yet.

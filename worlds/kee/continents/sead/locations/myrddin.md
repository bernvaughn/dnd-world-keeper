# Myrddin

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Russet. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** [Russet](../cities/russet/README.md).
- **Theme and peoples:** woodland region led by a Circle of the Moon druid; mixed peoples (orcs, dwarves, elves, humans, gnomes, centaurs).
- Led by the archdruid [Mythanar Sardan](../../../characters/npcs/mythanar-sardan.md) (Wood Elf, Circle of the Moon), who leads the [Circle of the Moon](../../../factions/circle-of-the-moon.md) and effectively runs Myrddin.
- Russet is the hub town, known for farming ("and yes, potatoes") and a famous Farmer's Market held Spring, Summer, and Fall that pulls people from all over.
- **Brantex, the Verdant Queen,** is rumored to be a 400-year-old green dragon living in the southern Myrddin woods. She is very aggressive and territorial.
- [Saltmaril](../cities/saltmaril/README.md), a gnomish port village on the southeastern coast where the forest meets the mountains, also lies in this region.

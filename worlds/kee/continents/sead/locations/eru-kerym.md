# Eru Kerym

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Keah. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** Keah.
- **Theme and peoples:** desert barbarians and monks.
- Little is recorded of the nation beyond the phrase "Desert Barbarians & Monks." It occupies the southern half of the eastern desert.

## Sub-locations

None named.

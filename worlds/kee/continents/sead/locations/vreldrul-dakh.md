# Vreldrul Dakh

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Dhaggil. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** Dhaggil, the orc capital, in the northwest of Sead.
- **Theme and peoples:** orc fields; primarily orcs.
- The region is named for the historic hero [Vreldrul Dakh](../../../gods/vreldrul-dakh.md), son of [Luthic](../../../gods/luthic.md), who in defiance of Gruumsh fought him in single combat, banishing his evil influence from this world. Cults of Gruumsh exist, but they receive no divine blessing, as he no longer has influence here.
- **Major tribes:** Wind Tusk, Dust Wind, Ordun Kud.
- **Minor tribes:** Flame Wolves, Azdrogh, Dradul, Dakh.

## Sub-locations

None named.

# Khenmeld

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Highbell. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** [Highbell](../cities/highbell/README.md).
- **Theme and peoples:** farming; primarily halflings (Highbell is 90% halfling, 7% gnome, 2% kender, 1% "Tall Folk").
- A gentle farming nation of orchards, rivers, and honey. Highbell (pop. 15,000) is described as "the most welcoming place in the realm."
- [Applewood Veil](applewood-veil.md) is a three-family orchard hamlet in the southwest of the region. Highbell sits at the far western tip of the southern lobe.

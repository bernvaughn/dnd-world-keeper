# Cliffspire

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Murkwallow. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** [Murkwallow](../cities/murkwallow/README.md).
- **Theme and peoples:** swamps; a bullywug empire with grung and other swamp animals.
- Ruled in practice by High Sentinel [Vorru-Kesh Blackreed](../../../characters/npcs/vorru-kesh-blackreed.md), who is in charge of Murkwallow.
- Murkwallow is a marsh metropolis that holds the only mithril and silver mines on Sead, making it economically entangled with nearly everyone: "The first [economy] makes Murkwallow beloved. The second makes Murkwallow dangerous."
- [Aestra](../cities/aestra/README.md), a dark gothic city of vampires in the northern mountains, also sits in this region. Its seat is [Castle Ael'Vorath](../cities/aestra/locations/castle-aelvorath.md), an ancient elven capital carved into the mountains.
- Home of "(Snail riders, Uzumaki)": a semi-abandoned idea parked here, to be expanded when the party heads toward Ardmont.

## Sub-locations

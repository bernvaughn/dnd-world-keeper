# Faircliff

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Bridges. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** [Bridges](../cities/bridges/README.md), capital of both Faircliff and Sead.
- **Theme and peoples:** rich nobles and merchants; Ardmont is 60% human, with orcs, elves, and others.
- **Richest region.** Technically independent, it is the seat of Bridges, the capital of both Faircliff and Sead.
- [Ardmont](../cities/ardmont/README.md), "The Sead of Greed," is a large industrial port city of machine and magic. It falls under the kingdom of Sead only barely: it has more power and influence than Bridges and the king, but falls in line for the greater good. It sits at the far western tip of Faircliff's northern coast, at the river mouth.

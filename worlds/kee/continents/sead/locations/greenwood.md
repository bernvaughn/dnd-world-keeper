# Greenwood

A logging camp at the western edge of Myrddin's forest, in [Myrddin](myrddin.md). Camp leader: [Garrick Tresman](../../../characters/npcs/garrick-tresman.md). From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## History

From Discord messages.

- In [session-2025-06-24-dm2](../../../sessions/session-2025-06-24-dm2.md), the party found Greenwood in ruin: several buildings burnt, every inhabitant dead in their homes, the sawmill's workhorses slain and half eaten, and a gnoll pack nearby. Dog-like paw prints led them there.
- In [session-2025-07-01-dm2](../../../sessions/session-2025-07-01-dm2.md), [Mabub](../../../characters/pcs/mabub.md) re-established the camp with ten hired hands, four draft horses, and the aid of [Aerin](../../../characters/npcs/aerin.md). The party cremated the dead, and Aerin grew an apple tree from the remains. [Ral Bosk](../../../characters/npcs/ral-bosk.md), a lumberjack with loose ties to the camp, was one of the hands. [Kip](../../../characters/npcs/kip.md) stayed behind later.
- Per the owner, Garrick Tresman and Sylves Forestrunner moved in after the camp was re-established. When is not recorded.
- A stone door found by following a map from Greenwood leads to [Thornrum](../cities/thornrum/README.md).

## Locations

- **Sylves Forestrunner's Roadside Camp Store:** a lean-to shopfront built onto a timber sorting yard: stacked logs, bark piles, a smokehouse, and a canvas awning over a counter. It has three stocks: food and travel bundles for loggers and road folk; leather repair and small tackwork; and archery and hunting resupply, where Sylves is quietly excellent. It smells like pine sap, smoked meat, and oiled leather. Prices are fair; some items are "limited" because Sylves is careful about what she brings out from her clan. Owner: [Sylves Forestrunner](../../../characters/npcs/sylves-forestrunner.md).

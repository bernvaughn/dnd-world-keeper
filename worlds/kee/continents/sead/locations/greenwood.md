# Greenwood

A logging camp at the western edge of Myrddin's forest, in [Myrddin](myrddin.md). Camp leader: Garrick Tresman. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## Locations

- **Sylves Forestrunner's Roadside Camp Store:** a lean-to shopfront built onto a timber sorting yard: stacked logs, bark piles, a smokehouse, and a canvas awning over a counter. It has three stocks: food and travel bundles for loggers and road folk; leather repair and small tackwork; and archery and hunting resupply, where Sylves is quietly excellent. It smells like pine sap, smoked meat, and oiled leather. Prices are fair; some items are "limited" because Sylves is careful about what she brings out from her clan. Owner: [Sylves Forestrunner](../../../characters/npcs/sylves-forestrunner.md).

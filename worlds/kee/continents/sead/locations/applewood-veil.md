# Applewood Veil

A small section of land in [Khenmeld](khenmeld.md), in the southwest of the region, settled by three families of halflings: "it isn't really a town, it is just 3 family orchards and a small shop on the side of the road that runs through it." From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## Overview

- Like spring in a photo: miles of orchards and green fields, a dirt road with a white picket fence running its entire length, goats, sheep, chickens, the occasional rabbit, and beehives.
- The three families are the Copperrabbit, Applefound and Heartbrand families (see [Applewood families](../../../factions/applewood-families.md)).
- **Mayor:** Sharmin Copperrabbit.
- **Sheriff:** Rister Heartbrand.

## Locations

- **Applewood Unveiled:** Belora Applebloom's small shop, filled with fresh produce; specializes in apple ciders, juices, sauces, and fresh apples.

# Eru Nailo

One of the seven regions (nations) of [Sead](../README.md). Capital: Allinas. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- **Capital:** Allinas.
- **Theme and peoples:** Desert Elven Kingdom.
- Home of the [Desert Elves](../../../species/desert-elf.md). It occupies the northern half of the eastern desert. Nothing else is recorded of the nation.

None named.

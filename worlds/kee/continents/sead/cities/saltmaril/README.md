# Saltmaril

A gnomish port village on the southeastern coast of Sead, in [Myrddin](../../locations/myrddin.md), where the forest meets the mountains. Its labeled map calls it "Saltmaril: Gnomish Port Village." From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

Cliffs and waterfalls rise behind the village, and an airship floats over it. Sites labeled on the map: Farmland, Temple of Callarduran (the gnome seed god, [Callarduran Smoothhands](../../../../gods/callarduran-smoothhands.md)), Workshop in the Mountain, and Fishing Piers. The map itself is not reproduced here.

## People

- [Captain Brindle "Boomfizz" Nackle](../../../../characters/npcs/brindle-nackle.md) and [Tikka Wrenchgut](../../../../characters/npcs/tikka-wrenchgut.md), gnome pirate/mercenaries and the adoptive parents of [Orla](../../../../characters/pcs/orla.md)


## History

From Discord messages.

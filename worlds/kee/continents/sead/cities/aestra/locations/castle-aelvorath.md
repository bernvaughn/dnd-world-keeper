# Castle Ael'Vorath

The seat of an immortal vampire lord in [Aestra](../README.md). From the Book of Sead 2.0.

Long before it became the seat of an immortal vampire lord, Ael'Vorath was the shining capital of an ancient elven kingdom carved into the mountains. The villagers still tend its gardens, smithies, vineyards, and markets, believing they serve an eternal royal house blessed by the gods. Beneath the great stone bridge lies the Hollow Pit, an abyss where countless undead sleep in perfect silence until their master calls. To outsiders, Ael'Vorath appears to be a thriving mountain city. Only those who descend into the darkness discover the kingdom's true population.

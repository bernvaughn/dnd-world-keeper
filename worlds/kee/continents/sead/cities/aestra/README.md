# Aestra

A dark gothic city of vampires in the northern mountains of [Sead](../../README.md), in [Cliffspire](../../locations/cliffspire.md). From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

The seat of the city is [Castle Ael'Vorath](locations/castle-aelvorath.md), an ancient elven capital carved into the mountains. The city's king is referenced ("The king is already dead," per a corpse's whisper to the undertaker Samir al-Hadid), and a vampire lord's fortress can be reached by an old military tunnel known to Darius al-Rahman. Their roles beyond these mentions are not recorded.


## Locations

- [The Crimson Vael](locations/the-crimson-vael.md): a tavern and bar
- [Castle Ael'Vorath](locations/castle-aelvorath.md): the vampire lord's seat

# Ardmont

A large industrial port city in [Faircliff](../../locations/faircliff.md), called "The Sead of Greed," with a mix of machine and magic. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

- Sits at the far western tip of the northern coast, at the river mouth. It holds more real power and influence than [Bridges](../bridges/README.md) and the king, but falls under the kingdom of Sead, barely, for the greater good.
- The arcane college and library take up a third of the city (central to north and west).
- Factories, warehouses, and the port take up another third along the entire eastern side.
- The southwestern third holds markets, most housing (low and high), churches and temples, and shops.
- The [Eldritch Knight Guard](../../../../factions/eldritch-knight-guard.md) maintains law and order under the council of the college, in Ardmont and on the island of Sead.
- The [Ren family](../../../../factions/ren-family.md) has a large home here and is the wealthiest family in Ardmont and on the island of Sead.
- Beaverfolk ([Castori](../../../../species/castori.md)) work on the docks as stevedores and factory hands; Ardmont is the Castori homeland.

## Demographics

60% Human, 20% Orc, 5% Elf, 5% Castori, 3% Dragonborn, 2% Goliath, 1% Halfling and Gnome, 1% Tiefling/Aasimar, about 3% other.

# Russet

The capital and hub town of [Myrddin](../../locations/myrddin.md), known for farming, "and yes, potatoes." From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

Its famous Farmer's Market, held Spring, Summer, and Fall, pulls people from all over to buy their stock. The region is led by [Mythanar Sardan](../../../../characters/npcs/mythanar-sardan.md) of the [Circle of the Moon](../../../../factions/circle-of-the-moon.md).

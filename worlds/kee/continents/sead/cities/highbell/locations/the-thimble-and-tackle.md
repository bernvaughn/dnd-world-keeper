# The Thimble & Tackle

A halfling bar at the end of the piers in [Highbell](../README.md), half on land and half on pilings over a slow slip of water. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## Details

- The floorboards "sing little sea-shanties when stepped on (purely from age, definitely not enchantment)."
- **Sign:** a thimble hooked on a fishing line, and beneath it, "LOW CEILINGS. HIGH SPIRITS."
- **Proprietor:** Maribel "Merry" Underbough.
- **House rules:** no weapons on tables (the table "gets jealous"); no duels before dessert; if you break a chair, you buy the chair a drink.
- A "FINE FOR DRAMA" jar (it's full); a stage exactly one halfling tall; a lost and found that is 90% daggers and 10% wedding rings.
- Signature menu item: Stew (Not Cursed), plus Trout Pie.
- The "Make It Right" Round costs 1 gp, with a bell-rung announcement by the bartender.
- Private Nook Rental is 2 sp/hour, with "plausible deniability."

# The Gull & Garland

A welcoming "big folk" tavern in [Highbell](../README.md), built specifically for visiting humans, half-elves, and other tallfolk. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## Building

45 ft x 30 ft, two stories, sea-grass thatch and salt-washed shingles. The sign is a painted gull holding a green garland in its beak. A wraparound porch like a ship's rail; a side door opens toward the piers.

- **Ground floor:** a common room with 10-foot ceilings and a big stone hearth with a copper kettle (today's "Dockside Stew"); a bar with a step-rail so halflings can lean and talk; a kitchen with a dumbwaiter; a "Wet Boots" mudroom whose rinsing trough has "NO CRABS" carved into it, "for reasons that are clearly recent"; and the Chart & Dice back room (house rule: no cheating unless it's funny).
- **Upstairs:** six guest rooms with halfling-sized "service hatches," and the Captain's Loft Suite with a floor-bolted lockbox and a "talk to the owner" bell pull.

# Highbell

The capital of [Khenmeld](../../locations/khenmeld.md) (pop. 15,000): a quaint halfling town beside a wide, lazy river that empties into a sparkling lake. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

The great bronze bell in the clocktower rings each dawn and dusk. Cobblestone streets wind between cozy burrow-houses built into grassy hills, each fronted by colorful gardens. The air always carries the scent of baked bread, pipe smoke, and wildflowers. Highbell is 90% halfling, 7% gnome, 2% kender, 1% "Tall Folk." It sits at the far western tip of the southern lobe of Sead. A town map exists in the source document but is not reproduced here.

## Districts

1. **Bellbank Wharf:** the heart of the fishing trade; dozens of squat fishing boats, gnomish contraptions for sorting and preserving the catch, colorful nets drying along the docks.
2. **The Greenfolds:** farmland and orchards surrounding the town: barley, onions, carrots, apples, and beekeeping producing Highbell's famous golden honey.
3. **Hillheart Borough:** the residential core: round-doored halfling burrows in grassy rises, connected by narrow stone bridges and winding paths.
4. **Gnomestead Lane:** a gnome-heavy neighborhood near the river, full of inventive workshops.
5. **The Tallward:** a small northern quarter built for "tall folk," with above-ground houses; locals joke it's "the place where doorframes have headaches."

## Culture

Highbellers are merry, kind-hearted, and nosy in the friendliest way; turning down offered food is considered rude.

- **Festival of the High Bell** (yearly): the great bell rings for a full minute at sunrise, then a town-wide feast with songs, games, and fireworks.
- **Bloom Festival** (summer harvest): flower garlands, music, pie contests.
- **Fishing Duels:** teams compete for the largest fish using comically oversized poles.
- **Kender Story Nights.**

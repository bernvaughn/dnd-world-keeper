# Thornrum

A hidden dwarven city behind a massive stone door with dwarven markings, found by following a map from [Greenwood](../../locations/greenwood.md). From Discord messages.

## Overview

- The door opens onto a long-dormant grand hall, more than a mile deep, with a second locked door whose seal was bricked in. Beyond it lies the city.
- Its guards wear distinctive masks and carry rifles.
- It has a temple of [Vergadain](../../../../gods/vergadain.md), where the priest [Jennora Firefound](../../../../characters/npcs/jennora-firefound.md) met the party.
- Its location is not recorded beyond lying behind Greenwood. [Vergadain's entry](../../../../gods/vergadain.md) says his dwarves sealed themselves off from the world 2,000 years ago; whether this is that city is not stated.
- First visited in [session-2025-07-01-dm2](../../../../sessions/session-2025-07-01-dm2.md).

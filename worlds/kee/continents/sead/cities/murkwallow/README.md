# Murkwallow

The marsh metropolis that is the capital of [Cliffspire](../../locations/cliffspire.md), the swamp empire of the bullywugs. From the Book of Sead 2.0.


## Overview

Murkwallow functions almost like an island in the swamp; it does not have "roads in" but permissions, currents, and pilots. It holds the only mithril and silver mines on Sead and is guarded by the [Gilded Reed Sentinels](../../../../factions/gilded-reed-sentinels.md). High Sentinel [Vorru-Kesh Blackreed](../../../../characters/npcs/vorru-kesh-blackreed.md) is in charge. The first economy (food and herbs) makes Murkwallow beloved; the second (metal) makes it dangerous. Its gods include [Ghurm, the Unblinking Steward](../../../../gods/ghurm.md), whose temples are the dikes, canals, reed farms, and mines.

## Access regions

- **The Outer Mires:** the sprawling swamp around the city: reed farms, eel traps and fish weirs, herb-gathering grounds, peat fields, watch platforms, stilt hamlets, shrine-mounds, and ancestor poles. The mires feed the city and protect it.
- **The Barge Approaches:** the main water routes in: deepwater barge lanes, toll pontoons, customs barges, floating beacon towers, pilot stations, rope-ferry crossings, and chain booms that can seal the approaches at night or during war.
- **The Dockring:** the outer ring of docks, floating warehouses, port authority offices, common taverns, labor hiring platforms, cranes, and fish markets where everyone enters.

## Main city island districts

- **Reedmarket:** broad commercial district of open-air stalls and raised timber streets: baskets, woven reeds, rope, mats, fishing gear, rain capes, marsh boots.
- **Lantern Court:** the social and diplomatic heart: large inns, guild guesthouses, music halls, feast courts, moneychangers, upscale taverns, contract houses. "If Reedmarket is the city's hands, Lantern Court is its smile."
- **Mosslane:** the herbalist, alchemical, and medicinal quarter: apothecaries, incense makers, healers, fungus growers, poison testers, herb auctions.
- **The Kiln Beds:** a hot industrial district on the firmest ground: pottery kilns, swamp-glass, lantern housings, ceramic pipework, seal jars.
- **Stilthouse Ward:** a broad residential-commercial quarter on stilts: raised homes, neighborhood shrines, family kitchens, local schools, stilt gardens, community halls, ferry slips.
- **Eelwharf:** the rougher dockside processing district: smoked eel, fish salting, pickling houses, bait sheds, cheap taverns, chandlers, rope yards, river crew hiring; "the stomach of the city."

## Metal and state districts

- **The Deep Assay:** the administrative center of the silver and mithril trade: assay halls, scale chambers, export licensing, tax desks, inspection vaults, official registries, dispute courts, anti-smuggling offices. Every legitimate ounce of precious metal passes through records here.
- **Vaultwater:** treasuries, counting houses, bonded storehouses, foreign trade compounds, armored barge berths; "where Murkwallow stores the consequences of being the only silver and mithril source on the continent."
- **Moonforge Ward:** the beating metallic heart: silver refiners, mithrilwright halls, mint houses, smelteries, enchanted workshops, elite armorers, furnace towers.
- **The Underpile:** at the edge of the city-island where ore arrives from the under-mire workings: ore barges, secured lifts, drainage towers, pump houses, mine intake tunnels; half industrial yard, half military checkpoint.

## Special regions

- **The Crown Mound:** the oldest, driest, most politically important rise: council halls, old shrines, noble guest residences, archives, ceremonial plazas, treaty chambers; the seat of civic rule and ceremonial religion.
- **The Lantern Pools:** shrines, bathhouses, tea courts, garden boardwalks over shallow reflective water: public festivals, wedding barges, moon-viewing platforms.
- **The Mireworks:** water control beyond the city core: pump houses, dredge docks, lock gates, canal engineers, waterwheel towers, emergency flood sirens; "Without the Mireworks, Murkwallow drowns."

## Social layers

- **The Friendly Face:** what visitors mainly see: the Dockring, Reedmarket, Lantern Court, parts of Stilthouse Ward, the Lantern Pools.
- **The Working Body:** what locals know: Eelwharf, Mosslane, the Kiln Beds, the Mireworks, the Underpile.
- **The Guarded Heart:** only the trusted access: the Deep Assay, Vaultwater, Moonforge Ward, the restricted Underpile, protected halls on the Crown Mound.
- **Wealthiest:** Crown Mound, Vaultwater, parts of Lantern Court, elite Moonforge edges. **Roughest:** Eelwharf, outer Dockring, Underpile labor quarters.

## Trade

- **Food exports:** smoked eel, pickled river fish, salted frog and marsh fowl, dried crawfish, marsh rice, water chestnuts, lotus root, spiced snail preserves, fermented lilybulb paste, blackwater fish sauce. They are cheap, durable, and travel well; bought by river crews, armies, caravans, and mining camps. Signature luxuries: Mooncap conserves, golden reed honey, pearled marsh caviar from giant swamp fish.
- **Herbal and alchemical exports:** fevermoss, bloodroot, marsh mint, bog sage, leechwort, silt nettle, willowbark, bitter reed resin, ghost lily petals, mireblossom paste; distilled frog venom, glowmoss extract, swamp resin, spore salts, preserved leeches, vapor bulbs, and dream lily essence ("a critical base component" for potions).
- **Metal economy:** the only mithril and silver mines on Sead: raw silver ore, refined ingots, coin silver blanks, jewelry-grade silver, silver wire and leaf, silvered weapon fittings, ceremonial temple silver. Mithril is much rarer and tightly controlled, mostly as licensed finished goods.
- The [Mithral Marsh Commission](../../../../factions/mithral-marsh-commission.md) runs the trade.

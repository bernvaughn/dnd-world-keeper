# Sead

An island continent divided into seven numbered regions (nations). Its capital is the city of Bridges in Faircliff, seat of the King of Sead, though Ardmont, also in Faircliff, holds more real power and influence than Bridges and the king. The numbering of the regions is not recorded. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## Geography

- Ardmont sits at the far western tip of the northern coast, at the river mouth.
- Highbell is at the far western tip of the southern lobe.
- Applewood Veil lies in the southwest.
- Dhaggil, the orc capital, is in the northwest.
- Greenwood is a logging camp at the western edge of Myrddin's forest.
- Saltmaril is on the southeastern coast, where the forest meets the mountains.
- Aestra lies in the northern mountains.
- The eastern desert splits into Eru Nailo in the north and Eru Kerym in the south.
- [Xianhua](../xianhua/README.md) is another continent, southeast of Sead.

## Events

1. [Kip](../characters/npcs/kip.md) decided to stay in Greenwood, "for totally plot related reasons." Jennora and the group headed south to [Saltmaril](../continents/sead/cities/saltmaril/README.md) to find pirates.
2. On the trip they encountered a [Free Tree](../characters/npcs/free-tree.md) and were transporting it attached to [Peat](../characters/npcs/peat.md).
3. Saltmaril is a very tiny sailing port. There they found [Captain Brindle](../characters/npcs/brindle-nackle.md) and [Tikka](../characters/npcs/tikka-wrenchgut.md), who were desperate for help sailing a bit south to recover an object called the [Obsidian Eye](../items/obsidian-eye.md). The party agreed to help Orla's old crew.
4. They set sail for [Ember Reef Island](../continents/sead/locations/ember-reef-island.md) on the gnomish ship [Sizzlewhip](../ships/sizzlewhip.md). They barely survived the trip, listing badly from a ruined keel, before being attacked by another group of pirates.
5. After defeating the pirates, they claimed the other ship and named it [Maboat](../ships/maboat.md). They landed on the island to repair the Sizzlewhip.
6. On the island they were attacked by a group of redcaps, whom the party defeated. The Obsidian Eye was in the possession of the redcap leader, [Grin-Gnash](../characters/npcs/grin-gnash.md).
7. Captain Brindle quickly accepted the artifact and revealed his deception: the Sizzlewhip could fly the entire time. He set off, then met the party back in Saltmaril and thanked them.
8. Tikka said that fitting the Maboat with elemental engines would cost 60,000 gp, or 15,000 gp per elemental engine (four engines).

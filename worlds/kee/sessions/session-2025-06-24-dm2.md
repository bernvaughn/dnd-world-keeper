## Events

1. The Heroes of Myrddin returned home to Russet to some fanfare and rest, but not for long.
2. [Mythanar](../characters/npcs/mythanar-sardan.md), Archdruid of Myrddin, came to Mabub for help. Myrddin was facing a lumber shortage and had not heard from [Greenwood](../continents/sead/locations/greenwood.md) in weeks. Mabub eagerly agreed to check on the lumbermill settlement.
3. Delta was gifted a donkey named [Peat](../characters/npcs/peat.md). Rhydelle, Orla, and Kip were loaded with hand pies for the three-day trip.
4. Along the way they found unusual dog-like paw prints. Greenwood was deathly quiet and in ruin, with several buildings burnt to the ground and every inhabitant dead, hiding in vain in their homes. Large, aggressive vultures attacked the party and were defeated.
5. At the sawmill they found the same devastation: a pile of workhorses slain and half eaten, and wagons loaded and ready for delivery. The saw was untouched.
6. Just after dark, a pack of vicious gnolls attacked and did not back down. They were defeated, but the viciousness of the attack was alarming.
7. The party decided to return to Russet and convey the sad news to Mythanar.

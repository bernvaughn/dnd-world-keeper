The Heroes of Myrddin travel to Bridges for the Tournament of Champions. Kee game. From Discord messages.

## Premise

The opening hook was posted on 5/10/2025. Eight months before, King Victor Vuldici grew ill, and after two months he died. His eldest son, Prince Vercani Vuldici, inherited the throne and decided to host a tournament of champions to bring his kingdom together during the transition. Each of "the eight regions" brings its best young team, representing the future growth of the kingdom. The reward is renown and a hefty purse for both the contestants and the region they represent.

The party was chosen to represent Myrddin and gathered in the hub town of [Russet](../continents/sead/cities/russet/README.md). The tournament was a week away and their royal escort had arrived to take them to the capital, [Bridges](../continents/sead/cities/bridges/README.md). They were put up at [The Nodding Dragon](../continents/sead/cities/russet/locations/the-nodding-dragon.md), a large inn about a mile and a half outside of town, and were leaving in the morning.

## Events

1. Mabub, Orla, Rhydelle, and Delta met at The Nodding Dragon, ate, slept, and relaxed before heading toward Bridges the next morning with their two Obsidian Knight escorts.
2. With limited hunting success, they arrived two days later in Bridges and were settled into guest accommodations in [Key Castle](../continents/sead/cities/bridges/locations/key-castle.md) (written "Castle Key" in the post).
3. Exploring the city, Delta made fast friends with a local merchant, [Wren Whistlestopper](../characters/npcs/wren-whistlestopper.md).
4. Orla met [Kip](../characters/npcs/kip.md), a small kobold. With some convincing and rules clarification, Kip was added as a fifth party member, despite not really being from Russet.
5. A large feast was held, with Prince [Vercani Vuldici](../characters/npcs/vercani-vuldici.md) giving a grand speech about the excitement of the tournament and hopes for growing prosperity.
6. The heroes took their rest before the games began the next day.

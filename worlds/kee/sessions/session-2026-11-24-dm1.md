# Session 2026-11-24

The first play session of the new Berksheland game, in the kee world. The characters were also created on this date (session 0), so the character files say "session 0 (2026-11-24)". It was not recorded whether session 0 and this play were one session or two; this file covers the play from the owner's notes.

## Party

- [Veralyn Fastfeet](../characters/pcs/veralyn-fastfeet.md)
- [Ryu Boku](../characters/pcs/ryu-boku.md)
- [Cornelius Tundrafluff](../characters/pcs/cornelius-tundrafluff.md)
- [Kessik Sunscale](../characters/npcs/kessik-sunscale.md) (NPC, travelling with them)

## Events

1. Veralyn, Ryu, and Kessik arrived in [Coldanchor](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md) on [The Stubborn Tern](../ships/the-stubborn-tern.md).
2. Veralyn stole food from the market, from [Dozy Dumplings](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/dozy-dumplings.md), without anyone noticing, and met Cornelius, who runs a snow cone stall there.
3. Ryu and Kessik took a job from [Alder Highdam](../characters/npcs/alder-highdam.md) to escort a [Bruinwood](../items/bruinwood.md) shipment (see the [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md) hook). Alder agreed to pay 150 gp on arrival of the shipment at the wharf. He also put them up in an inn for the night, [The Sleeping Anchor](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/the-sleeping-anchor.md).
4. Ryu and Kessik met Veralyn and Cornelius at the market, and they all tried snow cones.
5. Ryu, Kessik, and Veralyn went to the inn. Cornelius stayed with his mother, [Sherpa Tundrafluff](../characters/npcs/sherpa-tundrafluff.md).
6. Veralyn got a free room by deceiving the innkeeper, [Dagny Thornfeld](../characters/npcs/dagny-thornfeld.md), telling him she was on Alder's tab. She stole a fur blanket from the inn. Dagny has not noticed it missing, and the owner expects him to assume he misplaced it.
7. The party agreed to meet at dawn to begin traveling north, with Cornelius joining.

## Notes

- The pay is 150 gp on arrival of the shipment at the wharf. How it is split, the deadline, and the route are not recorded.
- Which of Veralyn and Cornelius also took the job is not recorded; Ryu and Kessik did.
- Both spellings aside, the owner confirmed "Ryu Boku" is the right name for Ryu.

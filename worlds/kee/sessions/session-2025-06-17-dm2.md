## Events

1. In the first round the party faced the halflings of Khenmeld (written "Khensmeld" in the post) and easily out-skilled them. [Marfina Copperrabbit](../characters/npcs/marfina-copperrabbit.md) was fatally injured by Mabub.
2. In the second round they faced the bullywug warriors of Cliffspire (written "Bullywog"). Some unique frog jumps put the warriors in a surprising position, but the heroes again out-skilled them. [Mobbeth](../characters/npcs/mobbeth.md) was fatally wounded by Mabub, and [Sir Gluff](../characters/npcs/sir-gluff.md) was exploded by lightning by Delta Lunascar. There was a delay in rounds to clean the arena.
3. On the last day they fought the champions of Faircliff in a nail-biter, and the Heroes of Myrddin outlasted and overcame them to win the tournament. It was held at the [Colosseum](../continents/sead/cities/bridges/locations/the-colosseum.md).
4. At the end of the contest the prince was officially crowned King [Vercani Vuldici](../characters/npcs/vercani-vuldici.md) of Sead.
5. Delta said her farewells to Wren, hoping to meet again, and the party began the trip home with a new companion, Kip of Russet ("or so he says").

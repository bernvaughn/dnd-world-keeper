## Events

1. Mabub decided to revive the settlement of Greenwood, partly as a business opportunity and partly because his family's welfare relies on it. [Mythanar](../characters/npcs/mythanar-sardan.md) gave his blessing, gifting the venture four draft horses and the aid of a young wildling, [Aerin](../characters/npcs/aerin.md).
2. Mabub posted in local taverns and job boards and found ten hands, including [Ral Bosk](../characters/npcs/ral-bosk.md), a skilled lumberjack with loose ties to Greenwood. The ten agreed to leave the next day.
3. On arriving, the party met a carrion crawler and made quick work of it. They started a pyre to cremate the fallen and cleaned the settlement as best they magically could, and did the same with the remains of the horses.
4. While searching, Delta found a map of the region and work tools for the sawmill.
5. After camping in Greenwood, they met a young elf druid who introduced himself as Aerin. He used the remains of the town and the fire to grow an apple tree in remembrance, and wildshaped on Delta's request.
6. Once the ten hands arrived and settled in, the party investigated markings on the map. Delta, who knows the language of the reclusive dwarves, identified them as dwarven and guided the party to a massive stone door with dwarven markings.
7. Mabub opened it with effort, using a shovel that ended up flat, and lifted the inside bar. Beyond was a long-dormant grand hall, stale and with inches of dust. Orla and Rhydelle led the way stealthily more than a mile deep to another locked door, which both rogues failed to unlock.
8. Delta unlocked it with her dwarven expertise and the rogues' guidance on the mechanics, but it would not budge. Mabub pushed it open through the door's bricked-in seal, revealing the hidden dwarven city of [Thornrum](../continents/sead/cities/thornrum/README.md).
9. Surprised dwarven guards in distinctive masks aimed rifles at the party. Delta communicated and negotiated, and they were led to a temple of Vergadain to meet a priest, [Jennora Firefound](../characters/npcs/jennora-firefound.md).
10. Initially guarded, Jennora grew interested when [SuperGold](../items/supergold.md) was mentioned. Mabub and Orla explained what it is and how to get it. She agreed to come to the surface and joined the party, which set off to find pirates who can process the rare gold for priests to enchant into SuperGold.

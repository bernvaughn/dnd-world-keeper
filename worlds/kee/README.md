# KEE: Keenan's Tuesday Game

Keenan's Tuesday game, set across the continents of Sead, Xianhua, and Berksheland. Sessions are played on Tuesdays.

## Setting

Sead is an island continent of seven nations, from the rich industrial port of Ardmont to the swamp empire of Murkwallow. Xianhua is a continent southeast of Sead with an oriental theme, known almost only from its map. Berksheland is a cold continent far north of both, home to the Ursine and to the Castori's Bruinwood digging. The gods are sorted into true gods, minor gods, demi-gods and "seed" gods from other realms. From the Book of Sead 2.0.

## House rules

From Discord messages. Posted 5/4/2025.

- Stats: 4d6, drop the lowest. If the overall +/- is less than +5, reroll if you wish.
- Initiative each round. If people don't like this after a few sessions, it will be dropped in favor of standard.
- No multiclassing without approval or a story beat.
- Roll HP each level with advantage (roll twice and take the highest).
- Flanking gives +2 to attack, not advantage.
- Third-party resources need approval.

## Character creation

From Discord messages. Posted 5/4/2025. The prompts players answered when making a character:

- What role does your family serve in the community? What brought your family to this region?
- A list of your family, with at least one basic thing about each.
- One friend, with at least one basic thing about them.
- One rival or enemy, and why they are a rival. It doesn't need to be heated or violent.
- Any deities or world information you want included. The post says players "will lose this when the game starts."
- One life goal or bucket-list item.
- Any alignment is fine, but the character has to work as a group and want to adventure.

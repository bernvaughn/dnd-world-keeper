# Bruinwood (Den-oak)

An ancient petrified wood that runs deep under the ice of [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md). It is especially useful for machine construction. The [Castori](../species/castori.md) call it Bruinwood; the [Ursine](../species/ursine.md) call it Den-oak. [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) is digging it up, which has caused tension between the Castori and the Ursine.

The Castori export a huge amount of it through the port of [Coldanchor](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md).

## Name ridges

Some Den-oak grows with ridges that spell Ursine names in an ancient Ursine language. Such a piece is very lucky if the name matches your own, and expecting mothers see it as a sign. The ridges occur naturally. Most Den-oak has no name ridges, but the Castori have been finding more and more pockets of it, and a bunch of it ended up in the shipment in the [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md) escort hook. One of those pieces bears Maren Voss's name, and probably several "Voss" ones.

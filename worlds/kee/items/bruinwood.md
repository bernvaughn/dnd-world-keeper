# Bruinwood (Den-oak)

An ancient petrified wood that runs deep under the ice of [Berksheland](../continents/berksheland/README.md). It is especially useful for machine construction. The [Castori](../species/castori.md) call it Bruinwood; the [Ursine](../species/ursine.md) call it Den-oak. [Gnawfast Excavation Co.](../factions/gnawfast-excavation-co.md) is digging it up, which has caused tension between the Castori and the Ursine.

The Castori export a huge amount of it through the port of [Coldanchor](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/README.md).

## Name ridges

Some Den-oak grows with ridges that spell Ursine names in an ancient Ursine language. Such a piece is very lucky if the name matches your own, and expecting mothers see it as a sign. The ridges occur naturally. The language is ancient, so only a few Ursine know what to look for and what the ridges mean. The Castori think those Ursine are making it up to be hostile toward them; to the Castori it is just excellent wood. Most Den-oak has no name ridges, but the Castori have been finding more and more pockets of it, and the pocket in the shipment was found in the buried ruins of an Ursine settlement in the north, and a bunch of it ended up in the shipment in the [Bruinwood Wharf](../continents/berksheland/cities/coldanchor/locations/bruinwood-wharf.md) escort hook. One of those pieces bears Maren Voss's name, and probably several "Voss" ones.

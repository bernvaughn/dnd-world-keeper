# Plumsworth's Hushstitch Leathers

Armor (leather armor), Common, requires no attunement (proposed). A beautiful set of leather armor from the personal stash of [Auntie Plumsworth](../characters/npcs/auntie-plumsworth.md), matron of [The Gilded Pantry](../factions/gilded-pantry.md). [Veralyn Fastfeet](../characters/pcs/veralyn-fastfeet.md) stole it, and is now on the run from the Pantry because of it.

## Properties (proposed)

The magic item and its Common rarity are confirmed by the owner. The effect below is a proposal the owner picked from three options.

While wearing it, you have Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to avoid being heard while moving across soft surfaces such as rugs, grass, and snow. It otherwise works as leather armor.

## Value

Common: 3d100 gp = 41 + 100 + 8 = 149 gp (owner's house rule).

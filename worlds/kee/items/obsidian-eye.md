# Obsidian Eye

An artifact. [Captain Brindle](../characters/npcs/brindle-nackle.md) and [Tikka](../characters/npcs/tikka-wrenchgut.md) wanted it recovered from a bit south of Saltmaril. The party found it on [Ember Reef Island](../continents/sead/locations/ember-reef-island.md) in the possession of the redcap leader [Grin-Gnash](../characters/npcs/grin-gnash.md), and Brindle accepted it. Its powers are not recorded. From Discord messages. See [session-2025-07-22-dm2](../sessions/session-2025-07-22-dm2.md).

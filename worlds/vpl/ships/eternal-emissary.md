# Eternal Emissary

A ship crewed by dwarves.

## Notes

- Captain: [Orion Darkwater](../characters/npcs/orion-darkwater.md).
- In [session-2025-11-09](../sessions/session-2025-11-09.md) it was held at a standstill at the [Neverwinter docks](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/docks.md) while its captain and Harbor Master [Karenaise](../characters/npcs/karenaise.md) disagreed over docking arrangements.
- It carried a shipment of [Blitzsteel ingots](../items/blitzsteel-ingots.md) bound for Adore Ore.
- Its dwarves also sold the party [adamantium](../items/adamantium.md) in a side deal.
- The type of ship is not recorded.

# The Cold Cup

A deserted, cursed ship.

## Notes

- Found by the party in [session-2025-08-03-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-08-03-dm1.md). It held the [tea set](../items/ilxaxergesh-tea-set.md) they were sent to retrieve.
- The ship itself was cursed. When [Zuri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md) grabbed the teapot, monsters made of bone, ship, and dead sailors awakened: skeletons, a bone and flesh golem, and a minotaur skeleton.
- The skeletons killed all the tortles from [Slow and Steady](slow-and-steady.md), and three of the four party members died.
- Presumed lost at sea.
- Where it came from, and the source of its curse, are not recorded.

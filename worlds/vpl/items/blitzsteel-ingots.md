# Blitzsteel Ingots

A shipment of ingots needed to craft the batch of [magic weapons](adore-ore-magic-weapons.md).

## Notes

- Shipped on the Eternal Emissary, captained by [Orion Darkwater](../characters/npcs/orion-darkwater.md), and held up at the [Neverwinter docks](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/docks.md) over a dispute with Harbor Master [Karenaise](../characters/npcs/karenaise.md).
- Delivered to [Adore Ore](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/adore-ore.md) by the dwarves, per contract, after the party negotiated reasonable dock fees.
- See [session-2025-11-09-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-11-09-dm1.md).

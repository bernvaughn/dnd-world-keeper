# Cracked Genie Lamp

A genie's lamp, cracked and powerless. The [Gobble-ins](../factions/flock-of-the-fallen-leaves.md) wished on it to become "powerful and ruthless goblins," and were turned into giant, sentient turkeys.

## Notes

- Connected to [Zhudee the Grand](../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md). The nature of the connection is not recorded.
- Tossed into a sinkhole at the [Gobble-in estate](../continents/faerun/locations/gobble-in-estate.md). In [session-2026-11-08](../sessions/session-2026-11-08.md), [Ember](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) found a harmless magical echo of the transformation there.

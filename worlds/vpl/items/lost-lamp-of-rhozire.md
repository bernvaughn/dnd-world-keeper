# Lost Lamp of Rhozire

A nonmagical but fancy oil lamp. A heirloom owned by the ancestors of [Myenceth Rhozire](../characters/npcs/myenceth-rhozire.md).

## Notes

- Held in the dwarf vault called the [Goldrat Hoard](../continents/faerun/locations/goldrat-hoard.md).
- In [session-2026-04-12](../sessions/session-2026-04-12.md), the party grabbed it and sprinted out as the cave collapsed, then returned it to Myenceth.

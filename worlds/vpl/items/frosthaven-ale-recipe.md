# Frosthaven Ale Recipe

A treasured brewing recipe, a piece of history in Neverwinter's brewing heritage. Its contents are not recorded.

## Notes

- Guarded by a bone golem in a cave beneath [Blackstone](../continents/faerun/locations/blackstone.md), where [Mortimer Graves](../characters/npcs/mortimer-graves.md) forced [Turdrag Hardbelt](../characters/npcs/turdrag-hardbelt.md) to brew from it. The party recovered it in [session-2025-04-13-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-04-13-dm1.md).
- Who holds it now is not recorded.

# Collector's Case

A collector's case holding a limited edition model train set. It was the Borquum family heirloom, together with the collector's trinkets.

## Notes

- Recovered by the party from the Borquum family's old house in an abandoned village, and returned to [Ilxaxergesh Borquum](../characters/npcs/ilxaxergesh-borquum.md) in [session-2025-05-04](../sessions/session-2025-05-04.md).
- Meant to be passed down to his son [Preltann Borquum](../characters/npcs/preltann-borquum.md).
- The village's location and the collector's identity are not recorded.

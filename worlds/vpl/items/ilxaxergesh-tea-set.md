# Ilxaxergesh's Tea Set

A tea set belonging to [Ilxaxergesh Borquum](../characters/npcs/ilxaxergesh-borquum.md). The cups are shaped like his head, and the teapot is shaped like a bronze dragon. Very exquisite but also tacky. The bronze dragon teapot is sentimental to him.

## Notes

- The party was sent to retrieve it in [session-2025-08-03-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-08-03-dm1.md).
- It was aboard [The Cold Cup](../ships/the-cold-cup.md), a cursed deserted ship. When [Zuri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md) grabbed the teapot, it awakened monsters made of bone, ship, and dead sailors. The set carried a curse, which the party learned about in play. The curse itself was not fleshed out.
- The party failed to retrieve it. It is presumed lost at sea with the ship.

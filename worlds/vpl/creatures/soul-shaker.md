# Soul Shaker

An undead amalgamation of arms from local folklore, long dismissed as a story to frighten disobedient children. Disembodied hands dig for something. It is not an official D&D creature, as far as a search turned up, and is treated here as homebrew.

## Lore

- Met in [session-2024-11-17-dm3](../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm3.md) in an abandoned farmhouse basement near [Godsbreath](../continents/faerun/cities/godsbreath/README.md), and again outside Neverwinter's gates in [session-2024-12-01-dm3](../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm3.md). The two encounters are the same individual, [the Godsbreath soul shaker](../characters/npcs/godsbreath-soul-shaker.md).
- A Guard bulletin says the way to end one permanently is to destroy all of its hands.
- Whether other soul shakers exist, what they dig for, and what causes the red-eyed possession in Godsbreath are not recorded.

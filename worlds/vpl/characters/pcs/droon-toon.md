Human Cleric, raised by dwarves. Player character. Member of the [Amethyst Guard](../../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- Bought 15 lbs of [adamantium](../../items/adamantium.md) from the dwarves of the Eternal Emissary: paid a 20 gp deposit, then the remaining 380 gp after delivery.
- Weapon chosen from [Nylit](../npcs/nylit.md): not recorded.

- In [session-2024-11-17-dm2](../../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm2.md), went to [Dis](../../continents/hell/cities/dis/README.md) in Hell with [Kronos](kronos.md) and [Egil Flametrap](egil-flametrap.md), and rescued [Labor](../npcs/labor.md) from the Mentiri.

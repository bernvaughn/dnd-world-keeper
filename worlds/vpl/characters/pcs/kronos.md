# Kronos

Known officially as Sir Kronos, and goes by Kronos as well. Player character. Member of the [Amethyst Guard](../../factions/amethyst-guard.md). Race and class are not recorded.

## Notes

- The spelling "Kronos" is official. Earlier, the owner recalled "Chronos" as the same character. That was an earlier recollection and is kept here for history.
- In [session-2026-04-12-dm1](../../sessions/session-2026-04-12-dm1.md), greedily grabbed a 1,000 gp diamond in the [Goldrat Hoard](../../continents/faerun/locations/goldrat-hoard.md), which caused the vault to collapse. Everyone escaped, and Kronos kept the diamond.
- In [session-2026-10-11-dm1](../../sessions/session-2026-10-11-dm1.md), went to the apple festival in [Bramblewick](../../continents/faerun/cities/bramblewick/README.md), fought rot-touched animated trees, and helped throw the false mayor in jail.

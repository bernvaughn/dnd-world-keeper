# Astrid Tealeaf

Female. Player character. Member of the [Amethyst Guard](../../factions/amethyst-guard.md). Race and class are not recorded.

## Notes

- Surname Tealeaf, from [session-2026-01-18](../../sessions/session-2026-01-18.md). Earlier sessions use "Astrid" only. The owner is not fully sure of the surname spelling, so it is to be compared against future session notes.
- In [session-2026-04-12](../../sessions/session-2026-04-12.md), upgraded her [rainbow rapier](../../items/rainbow-rapier.md) to +1 for 1,000 gp, and found 3 gp and a severed wood elf ear.
- In [session-2025-07-13](../../sessions/session-2025-07-13.md), landed in an orphanage during the Mad Hatter's dinner party, surrounded by orphans who blamed the party for eating all the cookies.
- In [session-2025-08-03](../../sessions/session-2025-08-03.md), robbed [The Old Grape Pharmacy](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/old-grape-pharmacy.md) with [Zuri](ziri.md) and left with 3 stolen antidote pills, which Astrid kept. Zuri then arrested Astrid as a staged cover.
- Sole survivor of the fight aboard [The Cold Cup](../../ships/the-cold-cup.md). Reached a deserted island, was rescued by a merchant ship, and returned to Neverwinter.
- In [session-2026-01-18](../../sessions/session-2026-01-18.md), helped clear and secure [Castle Coinfall](../../continents/faerun/locations/castle-coinfall.md) for [Borquum Holdings](../../factions/borquum-holdings.md).

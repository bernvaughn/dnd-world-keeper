# Egil Flametrap

Player character. Member of the [Amethyst Guard](../../factions/amethyst-guard.md). Race and class are not recorded.

## Notes

- Escaped the temple back to Neverwinter after [Roland](roland.md) was captured, and recovered at the Amethyst Guard.
- Spelling: the notes for [session-2024-11-24-dm1](../../sessions/session-2024-11-24-dm1.md) spelled the name "Egilflametap." The notes for [session-2024-12-08-dm1](../../sessions/session-2024-12-08-dm1.md) and [session-2025-05-04-dm1](../../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm1.md) spell it "Egil Flametrap," which the owner chose to use going forward.

- In [session-2024-11-17-dm2](../../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm2.md), went to [Dis](../../continents/hell/cities/dis/README.md) in Hell with [Droon Toon](droon-toon.md) and [Kronos](kronos.md), and rescued [Labor](../npcs/labor.md) from the Mentiri.

- Amaakos thanked "Egilflametap," along with [Maze](maze.md) and [Moress](moress.md), for enabling his escape, per an in-world report posted to Discord.

- In [session-2025-03-09-dm1](../../sessions/session-2025-03-09-dm1.md), helped set a trap in [Lumenshire](../../continents/faerun/cities/lumenshire/README.md) and stopped the [Prismatic Gnome Rangers](../../factions/prismatic-gnome-rangers.md).

# Amaakos

Tiefling explorer who likes to discover temples.

## Notes

- The party found his camp near the temple entrance, but he was not there.
- An in-world report posted to Discord calls him a famed spelunker. He was trapped in the temple by awakened serpent folk, and escaped when members of the Amethyst Guard attacked them to clear his route. He rescued Roland, and both reached town. See [session-2024-11-24-dm1](../../sessions/session-2024-11-24-dm1.md).
- The temple is [Szessith's Embrace](../../continents/faerun/locations/szessiths-embrace.md).
- Later escaped from the temple together with [Roland](../pcs/roland.md).

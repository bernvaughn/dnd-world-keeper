# Fizzwidget Brambleloop

Gnome wizard. Based in Neverwinter. His workshop's location is not recorded.

## Notes

- Lover of [Grakka Skullcruncher](grakka-skullcruncher.md). He wanted to bring her back after she died in battle and had her soul trapped in a cursed sword.
- Built a special iron golem to take the soul from the sword and give Grakka a body again. The party gathered three components for it: the Titanheart Core, the Breath Bellows, and the Binding Sigil Plate.
- Offered each party member 600 gp: 300 gp up front and 300 gp on success. He also gave them some additional money and a couple of potions after they brought the cores and bellows.
- The Heartforge ritual succeeded. Grakka lives as an Iron Orc and embraces him. See [session-2026-02-05](../../sessions/session-2026-02-05.md).

# Grakka Skullcruncher

Orc, now an Iron Orc. Lover of [Fizzwidget Brambleloop](fizzwidget-brambleloop.md).

## Notes

- Was a regular orc. She died in battle and had her soul trapped in a cursed sword. The sword's name is not recorded.
- While she destroyed a wizard tower, a sigil captured memories of her. The ruins became [The Driftspire](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/the-driftspire.md).
- The party fought her spirit there and won.
- After the Heartforge ritual succeeded, she was created anew as Grakka the Iron Orc and embraced Fizzwidget. See [session-2026-02-05](../../sessions/session-2026-02-05.md).

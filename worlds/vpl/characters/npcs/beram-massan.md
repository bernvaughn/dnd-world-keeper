# Beram Massan

Librarian. A recent hire to the senior board of librarians at the [Freedom Athenaeum](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/freedom-athenaeum.md). Race is not recorded.

## Notes

- Lost [The Creation of the Void](../../items/the-creation-of-the-void.md), which gave [Zhudee the Grand](zhudee-the-grand.md) his claim on the library. The book was a trap set by Zhudee to put the library in debt to him.
- Was in hot water for losing the book and angering Zhudee. The party found him and learned from him that the djinni had taken over the library.
- Was humiliated and cast out.

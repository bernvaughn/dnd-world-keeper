# Soul Shaker

An undead amalgamation of arms from local folklore, long dismissed as a story to frighten disobedient children. Hands dig for something. Not an individual with a name.

## Notes

- In [session-2024-11-17-dm3](../../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm3.md), [Mithran](../pcs/mithran.md) found one in an abandoned farmhouse basement near [Godsbreath](../../continents/faerun/cities/godsbreath/README.md). It killed [Roland](../pcs/roland.md), and the party won. The cause of the possession is not recorded.
- An Amethyst Guard bulletin from [session-2024-12-01-dm3](../../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm3.md) says a second party met the same creature outside Neverwinter's gates. So it survived or returned. To end it permanently, destroy all of its hands.

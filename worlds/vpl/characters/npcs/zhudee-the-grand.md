# Zhudee the Grand

Djinni. Owner of the luxury spa in the [Freedom Athenaeum](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/freedom-athenaeum.md) in Neverwinter.

## Backstory

Established by the owner after the sessions.

- Was trapped in a lamp in ruins. The ruins are unnamed and their location is not recorded. Who sealed him in, and for how long, are not recorded.
- A bandit gang found the [lamp](../../items/cracked-genie-lamp.md) and got three wishes: a feast, then to live as powerful goblins so they would never pay for food again, then to free the genie. The gang became the [Gobble-ins](../../factions/flock-of-the-fallen-leaves.md).
- [Pip](pip.md) convinced the rest of the flock to free him. They agreed easily because they wanted nothing else. The lamp cracked when he was freed, and the flock threw it into a sinkhole for fun.
- After he was freed, he went on to start his spa business. The spa has entrances in Neverwinter, including the [Freedom Athenaeum](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/freedom-athenaeum.md), but exists on a different plane. Which plane, and whether he still knows the Gobble-ins, are not recorded.

## Notes

- Took over the library and turned it into a spa. His claim came from [Beram Massan](beram-massan.md) losing his prized book, [The Creation of the Void](../../items/the-creation-of-the-void.md), which was a trap to put the library in debt to him.
- Amused by [CLAM](../pcs/clam.md)'s sneaky deal for a free exit, and let him leave with a grain of sand in exchange for 4 peppers.
- Made a deal with [Aramai](../pcs/aramai.md) and [Brute](../pcs/brute.md): he is owed another business to add to his domain as an entrance to the spa, and in exchange he will reopen the library as part of his business. **Open thread:** the debt is still unpaid.
- Met [Tondin Hallowflower](tondin-hallowflower.md) about a deal to supply aromatics to the spa.
- Offers Amethyst Guard members perks at the spa. See the Freedom Athenaeum file.
- The [cracked genie lamp](../../items/cracked-genie-lamp.md) found in [session-2026-11-08-dm1](../../sessions/session-2026-11-08-dm1.md) was his prison. See Backstory.
- His other holdings beyond the spa are not recorded.

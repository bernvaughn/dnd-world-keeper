# Myenceth Rhozire

Copper dragonborn collector. Pronounced "Men-keth Rho-zie-er."

## Notes

- Looked for adventurers to find the [Lost Lamp of Rhozire](../../items/lost-lamp-of-rhozire.md), an heirloom owned by his ancestors. The party recovered it and he paid them in coins. See [session-2026-04-12](../../sessions/session-2026-04-12.md).
- Rival collector of [Ilxaxergesh Borquum](ilxaxergesh-borquum.md). This lore was established by the owner after the session, not from play.
- Where he lives is not recorded.

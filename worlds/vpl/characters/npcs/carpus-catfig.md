# Carpus Catfig

Human trickster, based on the Mad Hatter. Lives in Neverwinter.

## Personality

Simply crazy, and likes to have fun.

## Notes

- Captured the party at his exclusive dinner party, and fitted them with ankle bracelets that teleported them back to his table at each meal. See [session-2025-07-13-dm1](../../sessions/session-2025-07-13-dm1.md).
- When [Ember Flamethrower](../pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) refused to eat and broke free, he attacked, with two polar bears.
- Killed by Ember.

# Ilxaxergesh Borquum

Old dragonborn who lives in Neverwinter. Pronounced "Ill-zaxs-ergesh."

## Notes

- Father of [Preltann Borquum](preltann-borquum.md). He wanted to pass a family heirloom to his son before he dies.
- Hired the party to recover it from the Borquum family's old, ruined house.
- Was thrilled to receive both the [collector's case](../../items/collectors-case.md) and the collector's trinkets, and paid an extra 500 gp.
- His servant and housekeeper is [Mashid](mashid.md).
- In [session-2025-08-03](../../sessions/session-2025-08-03.md), he wanted the party to retrieve his [tea set](../../items/ilxaxergesh-tea-set.md), with cups shaped like his head and a bronze dragon teapot. He offered no pay, and put the party's cruise on his tab with [Bring-Your-Own-Ship Cruises](../../factions/bring-your-own-ship-cruises.md), a company he works with a lot. As far as he knows, the ship was lost and the party was defeated.
- Rival collector of [Myenceth Rhozire](myenceth-rhozire.md), another dragonborn collector. This lore was established by the owner after the sessions, not from play.
- His business is [Borquum Holdings](../../factions/borquum-holdings.md). In [session-2026-01-18](../../sessions/session-2026-01-18.md), the party investigated one of its toll roads and secured [Castle Coinfall](../../continents/faerun/locations/castle-coinfall.md) for it.

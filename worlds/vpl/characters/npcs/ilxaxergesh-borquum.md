# Ilxaxergesh Borquum

Old dragonborn who lives in Neverwinter. Pronounced "Ill-zaxs-ergesh."

## Notes

- Father of [Preltann Borquum](preltann-borquum.md). He wanted to pass a family heirloom to his son before he dies.
- Hired the party to recover it from the Borquum family's old, ruined house.
- Was thrilled to receive both the [collector's case](../../items/collectors-case.md) and the collector's trinkets, and paid an extra 500 gp.
- His servant and housekeeper is [Mashid](mashid.md).

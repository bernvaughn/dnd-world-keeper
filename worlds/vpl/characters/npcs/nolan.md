# Nolan

Warlock in service of the devil prince [Mammon](mammon.md). Formerly head of the town of [Smelton](../../continents/faerun/cities/smelton/README.md). Race and pronouns are not recorded.

## Notes

- In [session-2025-03-02-dm3](../../sessions/session-2025-03-02-dm3.md), warned the [Amethyst Guard](../../factions/amethyst-guard.md) that all previous attempts to deal with the mine's threat ended with no one returning.
- The party learned from [Petro](petro.md) that Nolan had been corrupting the land and exploiting the townspeople. They ran him out of town and gave his wealth to the miners.
- Where he went is not recorded.
- In [session-2025-06-08-dm3](../../sessions/session-2025-06-08-dm3.md), was recognized in the port of Neverwinter as a warlock in service of the devil prince [Mammon](mammon.md) and the villain behind the port's increased fees. The report calls him a "familiar face," and the owner confirmed it is the same Nolan as in Smelton.

# Mara Bramble

Witch. Lives outside [Bramblewick](../../continents/faerun/cities/bramblewick/README.md). Her house's location is not recorded.

## Notes

- Saved the town's orchards from blight under a contract with the mayor: a total of 20,000 gp, with 10,000 gp up front and 10,000 gp due at the first picking of sound fruit.
- When the balance went unpaid, she withdrew her blessing, which made all the apples, and all food made with them, rot. The contract allowed this.
- The party fought rot-touched animated trees on the way to her house. There she gave [Strange](../pcs/strange.md) cookies and gave her side of the story. See [session-2026-10-11-dm1](../../sessions/session-2026-10-11-dm1.md).
- The apples never recovered.

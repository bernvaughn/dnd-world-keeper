# Pink Ranger

Gnome, one of the [Prismatic Gnome Rangers](../../factions/prismatic-gnome-rangers.md). Real name and pronouns are not recorded.

## Notes

- The elusive one who escaped when the Guard defeated the other Rangers in [session-2025-03-09-dm1](../../sessions/session-2025-03-09-dm1.md).
- Probably the gnome who later put a contract on [Ember Flamethrower](../pcs/ember-flamethrower.md), which the party found at The Akward Canvas in [session-2025-05-04-dm1](../../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm1.md). That session called the gnome the "pink ranger."

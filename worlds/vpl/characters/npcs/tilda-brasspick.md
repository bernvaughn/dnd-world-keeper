# Tilda Brasspick

A miner who took the place of the mayor of [Bramblewick](../../continents/faerun/cities/bramblewick/README.md) under the name Alden Whitlock.

## Notes

- Killed Alden Whitlock and took his place as mayor, after his father Edric Whitlock, the previous mayor, died.
- Refused to pay the remaining fee owed to the witch [Mara Bramble](mara-bramble.md), which led to all the town's apples rotting.
- The party learned who the mayor really was and threw the mayor in jail. See [session-2026-10-11-dm1](../../sessions/session-2026-10-11-dm1.md).
- Race and appearance are not recorded.

# David Davidson

A former player character who turned on the [Amethyst Guard](../../factions/amethyst-guard.md) and is now an NPC. Race and class are not recorded. He. The spelling is unconfirmed. The in-world report for [session-2025-07-13-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-07-13-dm2.md) calls him "David Davidson Jr." The owner confirmed it is the same character, so "Jr" is a variant of the name.

## Notes

- No longer affiliated with the Guard and "considered hostile and dangerous," per the memo in [session-2025-05-04-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm2.md). Established by the owner after the session.
- In [session-2025-07-13-dm2](../../sessions/session-2025-07-13-dm2.md), fought seven Guard members in a warehouse in an extended fight, revealed the location of the transport wagons, and was begrudgingly released short one [Wand of Fireball](../../items/wand-of-fireball.md).
- What he did to turn on the Guard is not recorded.

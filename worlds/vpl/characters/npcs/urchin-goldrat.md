# Urchin Goldrat

Dwarf fisherman. Hangs out at the [Salty Shackle](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/salty-shackle.md) in Neverwinter.

## Notes

- Branded with a map on his chest that marks the location of the [Goldrat Hoard](../../continents/faerun/locations/goldrat-hoard.md) in the forest. The map is incomprehensible until an observer says "I promise to take nothing that does not belong to me from the Goldrat Hoard."
- The party bribed him with booze to come with them. He slept drunkenly through most of the adventure. See [session-2026-04-12-dm1](../../sessions/session-2026-04-12-dm1.md).
- The notes also call him a gladiator and a bandit. This is the same person. The details are not recorded.
- The party left him to go on his way afterward.

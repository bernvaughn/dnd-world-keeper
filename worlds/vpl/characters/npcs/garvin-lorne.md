Missing cousin of the merchant [Olgar Greenthorn](olgar-greenthorn.md). The report also calls Olgar his uncle, so the relation is unconfirmed. Race is not recorded. He, per the report.

He admitted it was his idea to steal the loot, but [Serina the Shadowbinder](serina-the-shadowbinder.md) deceived him and betrayed him once he was no longer needed.
- Was returned to Olgar relatively unharmed.

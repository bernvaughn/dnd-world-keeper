# Vessarith Ashquill

Bone devil. Headmaster of the [Collegium Infernalis](../../factions/collegium-infernalis.md) in Infernus, and presumably the holder of its contract with [Candlemere Stables](../../continents/faerun/locations/candlemere-stables.md).

## Personality

Polite and exacting. He treats a missing librarian as a breach of contract, not an emergency.

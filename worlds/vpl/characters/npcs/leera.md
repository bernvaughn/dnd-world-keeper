# Leera

Male high elf. Proprietor of [The Akward Canvas](../../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/akward-canvas.md) in Neverwinter.

## Personality

Elitist. Not an artist and not a demon dealer: he simply got wrapped up in a demon situation.

## Notes

- Source of [demonic blood](../../items/demonic-blood.md) for the party.
- [Ember Flamethrower](../pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) punched him in the throat and took the blood. [Clovis](../pcs/clovis.md) then intimidated him into not telling anyone.

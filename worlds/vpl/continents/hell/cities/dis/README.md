# Dis

A smoldering, rusty city deep in Hell. A different city in the same Hell as Infernus, where the Collegium Infernalis is based. Its rulers and other details are not recorded.

## Notes

- In [session-2024-11-17-dm2](../../../../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm2.md), [Droon Toon](../../../../characters/pcs/droon-toon.md), [Kronos](../../../../characters/pcs/kronos.md), and [Egil Flametrap](../../../../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md) went here with a lead from [Lord Pezz](../../../../characters/npcs/lord-pezz.md), avoided its dangers, traps, and temptations, and rescued [Labor](../../../../characters/npcs/labor.md) from the Mentiri.

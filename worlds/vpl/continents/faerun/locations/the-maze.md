# The Maze

An unnamed maze on the road to Neverwinter. Its name, owner, and exact location are not recorded. "The Maze" is a placeholder.

- Bean stalks attack inside.
- In [session-2024-12-01-dm4](../../../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm4.md), [Jax](../../../characters/pcs/jax.md), [Faelyn](../../../characters/pcs/faelyn.md), [Findak](../../../characters/pcs/findak.md), and [Cohen](../../../characters/pcs/cohen.md) entered and were attacked.

# Castle Coinfall

A fort and castle with a toll booth on a toll road, about a day's travel from Neverwinter. Held by [Borquum Holdings](../../../factions/borquum-holdings.md). The name was chosen after the party's visit.

## Areas

- **Toll booth:** where the party found a dead dwarf, who exploded, and took some silver pieces from the tolls.
- **Castle:** entered by the party in [session-2026-01-18](../../../sessions/session-2026-01-18.md). It was full of traps, set by a drider that wanted the castle. A mimic was also inside.
- **Courtyard:** flooded with water by a lever. An illusion of a dwarf tried to get the party to pull it.

## Notes

- The toll road's name is not recorded.
- The drider and the mimic were killed, and the party secured the fort for Borquum Holdings.
- The two dwarves, one in the toll booth and one an illusion, are not named.

## Characters

- [Droon Toon](../../../characters/pcs/droon-toon.md), [Astrid Tealeaf](../../../characters/pcs/astrid.md), [Kronos](../../../characters/pcs/kronos.md), [Strange](../../../characters/pcs/strange.md), and [Ember Flamethrower](../../../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) cleared and secured it.

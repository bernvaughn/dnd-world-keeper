# Candlemere Stables

An island library near Neverwinter. Its exact location is not recorded. The name was chosen after the party's visit.

## Notes

- Run by head librarian [Maelis Quillmere](../../../characters/npcs/maelis-quillmere.md), under a contract with the [Collegium Infernalis](../../../factions/collegium-infernalis.md) that lets the college's devils borrow any book they request.
- In [session-2026-09-13](../../../sessions/session-2026-09-13.md), the librarian disappeared and a ghost gorgon attacked the library, petrifying her and [Strange](../../../characters/pcs/strange.md). The party took [Tide and Tardy](../../../ships/tide-and-tardy.md) to the island, served books to the devils, killed monsters, and defeated the ghost gorgon. The librarian resumed library operations.
- Whether it is related to the [Freedom Athenaeum](../cities/neverwinter/locations/freedom-athenaeum.md) is not recorded.

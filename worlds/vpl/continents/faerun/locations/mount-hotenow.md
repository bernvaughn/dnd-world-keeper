# Mount Hotenow

A mountain. Its location is not recorded. The Amethyst Guard scouted its base as a potential site for the new Amethyst Keep.

## Notes

- In [session-2024-12-01-dm2](../../../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm2.md), five Guard members scouted a potential location at its base. They fought a bulette, which killed [Lady Arka](../../../characters/pcs/lady-arka.md), freed a fire elemental from a magma prison, and found an [electric bow](../../../items/electric-bow.md).

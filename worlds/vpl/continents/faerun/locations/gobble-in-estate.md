# Gobble-in Estate

Home of the [Flock of the Fallen Leaves](../../../factions/flock-of-the-fallen-leaves.md): two modest farmhouses and a guest house in the woods south of Neverwinter. Its exact location is not recorded.

## Notes

- A snare trap was set on the path in [session-2026-11-08](../../../sessions/session-2026-11-08.md).
- A sinkhole nearby holds the [cracked genie lamp](../../../items/cracked-genie-lamp.md).
- Gobb's farmhouse, Cluckbane's farmhouse, the guest-house infirmary, and the guest-house pantry each held loot. See the session file.
- Characters: [Gobb the Red](../../../characters/npcs/gobb-the-red.md), [Cluckbane](../../../characters/npcs/cluckbane.md), [Pip](../../../characters/npcs/pip.md), [Auntie Brindle](../../../characters/npcs/auntie-brindle.md).

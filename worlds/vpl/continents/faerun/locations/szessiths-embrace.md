# Szessith's Embrace

An ancient subterranean temple of Szessith the Coiled Dark, a long-forgotten serpent deity, and the base of the [Dark Embrace](../../../factions/dark-embrace.md). Generated after the session and approved by the owner. Its exact location is not recorded. The party traveled from Neverwinter.

## Notes

- Held a pit room with snakes, oil, and vines, and guards. Serpent folk awakened inside.
- In [session-2024-11-24-dm1](../../../sessions/session-2024-11-24-dm1.md), [Roland](../../../characters/pcs/roland.md), [Maze](../../../characters/pcs/maze.md), [Moress](../../../characters/pcs/moress.md), and [Egil Flametrap](../../../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md) explored it. [Amaakos](../../../characters/npcs/amaakos.md) had camped near the entrance and was trapped inside.

# Gravelgaze Shrine

An earth elemental shrine in Neverwinter. The name was chosen after the party's visit.

## Notes

- In [session-2026-02-05-dm1](../../../../../sessions/session-2026-02-05-dm1.md), the party ruined the shrine to awaken its guardians, earth elementals, then extracted their cores to craft a Titanheart Core for [Fizzwidget Brambleloop](../../../../../characters/npcs/fizzwidget-brambleloop.md).
- The elementals gave 3,000 sp, 200 gp, and two 100 gp gems. The fight was tough, and the party won.
- The shrine is ruined. Its exact location in Neverwinter is not recorded.

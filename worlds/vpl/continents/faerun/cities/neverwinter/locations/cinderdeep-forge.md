# Cinderdeep Forge

An ancient dwarven forge in Neverwinter, inhabited by soot mephits. The name was chosen after the party's visit.

## Notes

- Associated with [Mordin](../../../../../characters/npcs/mordin.md), a dwarven god.
- The mephits keep stealing tools. In [session-2026-02-05-dm1](../../../../../sessions/session-2026-02-05-dm1.md), the party needed tools to extract the Breath Bellows from the ruined magical forge, and avoided the mephits, thinking they were imps.
- The bellows went to [Fizzwidget Brambleloop](../../../../../characters/npcs/fizzwidget-brambleloop.md).
- Its exact location in Neverwinter is not recorded.

# The Driftspire

A ruined wizard tower in Neverwinter, with floating debris. The name was chosen after the party's visit.

## Notes

- [Grakka Skullcruncher](../../../../../characters/npcs/grakka-skullcruncher.md) destroyed the tower, and sigils there captured memories of her.
- In [session-2026-02-05](../../../../../sessions/session-2026-02-05.md), the party recovered the sigils and formed them into a Binding Sigil Plate for [Fizzwidget Brambleloop](../../../../../characters/npcs/fizzwidget-brambleloop.md). They fought Grakka's spirit here and won, receiving the [Thunderous Mace](../../../../../items/thunderous-mace.md).
- The wizard who owned the tower is not recorded. Its exact location in Neverwinter is not recorded.

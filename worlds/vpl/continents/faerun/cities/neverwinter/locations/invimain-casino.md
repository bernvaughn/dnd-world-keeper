# Invimain Casino

An underground casino in Neverwinter that hosts a fighting tournament. The spelling "Invimain" is from the report. Its owner and exact location are not recorded.

## Notes

- In [session-2025-04-13-dm3](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-04-13-dm3.md), Amethyst Guard members won a tournament here. They fought duergar, an owlbear, and a necromancer with zombies. The final opponent was their own guide to the casino, who had run a long con to win a bet on the challengers.
- The guide is unnamed and has no file.

# The Akward Canvas

Shop in Neverwinter. The spelling "Akward" is intentional.

## Proprietor

- [Leera](../../../../../characters/npcs/leera.md): male high elf and shop proprietor

## Notes

- Source of [demonic blood](../../../../../items/demonic-blood.md), which the party obtained relatively easily here in [session-2025-11-09-dm1](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-11-09-dm1.md).
- The third of three shops broken into by goblin burglars in [session-2024-12-08-dm1](../../../../../sessions/session-2024-12-08-dm1.md).
- In [session-2025-05-04-dm1](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm1.md), the party brought the captured assassin [Ishaxa](../../../../../characters/npcs/ishaxa.md) here and found a contract on [Ember Flamethrower](../../../../../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md). They left on good terms with Leera.
- Connected to the [demons from the Akward Canvas](../../../../../factions/demons-from-the-akward-canvas.md), the Amethyst Guard's name for them.
- The in-world report for that session calls it "a front for a secretive guild, run by Leera." The owner confirmed the guild is the demons. The report spells the shop "Awkward," a common typo of "Akward."
- What the shop sells is not recorded.

# Blood Orc's Fist

Stadium and gladiator arena in Neverwinter.

## Notes

- Has a deal with [Adore Ore](adore-ore.md) to supply minotaur leather. The [Tribe of the Last Charge](../../../../../factions/tribe-of-the-last-charge.md) sends minotaurs who wish to die in combat and be made into weapons.
- In [session-2025-11-09](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-11-09.md), the most recent seven minotaurs became the reigning champions, and the party had to defeat them to get the leather.
- The party was paid 500 gp to promote the fight and draw a crowd for that night, and signed up to fight the minotaurs.
- Bets were placed on the fight and paid out at 2:1.

## Characters

- [Rattarack](../../../../../characters/npcs/rattarack.md): the biggest of the seven minotaur champions

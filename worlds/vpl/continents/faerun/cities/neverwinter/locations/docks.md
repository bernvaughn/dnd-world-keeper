# Neverwinter Docks

The harbor of Neverwinter.

## Characters

- [Karenaise](../../../../../characters/npcs/karenaise.md): female centaur, Harbor Master
- [Captain Orion Darkwater](../../../../../characters/npcs/orion-darkwater.md): dwarf captain of the Eternal Emissary

## The Eternal Emissary

A ship crewed by dwarves. In [session-2025-11-09](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-11-09.md) it was held at a standstill at the docks while its captain and the Harbor Master disagreed over docking arrangements. It carried a shipment of [Blitzsteel ingots](../../../../../items/blitzsteel-ingots.md) bound for Adore Ore, and the ship's dwarves also sold the party [adamantium](../../../../../items/adamantium.md) in a side deal.

## Notes

- The party negotiated the Harbor Master down to reasonable dock fees. The dwarves paid and delivered the ingots.

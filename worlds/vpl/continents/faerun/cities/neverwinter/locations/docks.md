# Neverwinter Docks

The harbor of Neverwinter.

## Characters

- [Karenaise](../../../../../characters/npcs/karenaise.md): female centaur, Harbor Master
- [Captain Orion Darkwater](../../../../../characters/npcs/orion-darkwater.md): dwarf captain of the Eternal Emissary
- [Golric](../../../../../characters/npcs/golric.md): was squatting in the Cotswald here

## Ships

- [Eternal Emissary](../../../../../ships/eternal-emissary.md): a dwarf-crewed ship held at a standstill here in [session-2025-11-09](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-11-09.md) over a dispute about docking arrangements. The party negotiated the Harbor Master down to reasonable dock fees, and the dwarves paid and delivered their [Blitzsteel ingots](../../../../../items/blitzsteel-ingots.md). The ship's dwarves also sold the party [adamantium](../../../../../items/adamantium.md).
- [Cotswald](../../../../../ships/cotswald.md): a sloop that [Golric](../../../../../characters/npcs/golric.md) was squatting in. Secured for the [Amethyst Guard](../../../../../factions/amethyst-guard.md) in [session-2024-10-13](../../../../../sessions/session-2024-10-13.md).

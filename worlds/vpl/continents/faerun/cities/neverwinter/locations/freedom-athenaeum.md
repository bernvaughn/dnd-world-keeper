# Freedom Athenaeum

One of Neverwinter's great libraries, once the city's most prestigious. [Zhudee the Grand](../../../../../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md) took it over and turned it into a luxury spa.

## The spa

- The spa's name appears as "Freedom Athenaeum Spa" and "Free Athenius Spa" in the owner's promotional material. Both are recorded as variants, to be compared against future session notes.
- Inside the building is an island above a swirling whirlpool, a planar trick, with a platform that overlooks it.
- Unnamed water elemental staff greet visitors as concierge.
- The spa's aromatics come from [Hallowflower Aromatics](hallowflower-aromatics.md).
- The library's books are in safekeeping, not destroyed.

## Amethyst Guard perks

Members of the [Amethyst Guard](../../../../../factions/amethyst-guard.md) are offered one free foot soak, half-price aromatic steam sessions, and priority booking for a planned Library Lounge. The Library Lounge is promised once the Guard completes its arrangement with Zhudee. See the open thread in [session-2025-09-14](../../../../../sessions/session-2025-09-14.md).

## Characters

- [Zhudee the Grand](../../../../../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md): djinni and owner of the spa
- [Beram Massan](../../../../../characters/npcs/beram-massan.md): librarian who lost Zhudee's book and was cast out
- [Tondin Hallowflower](../../../../../characters/npcs/tondin-hallowflower.md): alchemist who supplies the spa's aromatics

# Godsbreath

A town upriver from Neverwinter. Which river, and how far, are not recorded. Not much else is recorded.

## Overview

- Held local festivities when the party visited.
- Farmers with solid red eyes, possessed, attacked the town. It needed adventurers' help, and offered building supplies, food, and skilled carpenters in exchange. See [session-2024-11-17-dm3](../../../../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm3.md).
- Local folklore tells of the soul shaker, an undead amalgamation of arms, long dismissed as a story to frighten disobedient children. The party found one in an abandoned farmhouse basement.

## Leadership

- [Auntie Deanna](../../../../characters/npcs/auntie-deanna.md): local leader.
- [Kiarra](../../../../characters/npcs/kiarra.md): Deanna's goddaughter, an investigator.

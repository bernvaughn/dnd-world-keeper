# Bramblewick

A town east of Neverwinter, known for its orchards and apple festival.

## Overview

- About 1,000 residents. Roughly 3,000 people attend the apple festival.
- Its orchards were saved from blight by the witch [Mara Bramble](../../../../characters/npcs/mara-bramble.md). When the mayor failed to pay the remaining fee, she withdrew her blessing and all the apples rotted. They never recovered. See [session-2026-10-11](../../../../sessions/session-2026-10-11.md).

## Leadership

- **Mayor:** Alden Whitlock, really [Tilda Brasspick](../../../../characters/npcs/tilda-brasspick.md), a miner who killed Alden and took his place. Jailed by the party.
- **Previous mayor:** Edric Whitlock, Alden's father, who has died.
- **Acting mayor:** [Sella Vane](../../../../characters/npcs/sella-vane.md), elf town clerk.

## The mayor's advisors

- Merrit Fenlow: halfling orchardmaster
- [Sella Vane](../../../../characters/npcs/sella-vane.md): elf town clerk
- Dunstan Pike: dwarf festival steward
- Maudra Quince: half-orc village healer

## The apple festival

Activities before the disaster: apple-bobbing, pie-and-cider contest, orchard obstacle race, caramel-apple decorating, cider-press challenge, harvest-mask parade, guessing barrel, and a fiddler's circle.

## Notes

- Rot-touched animated trees were found near the orchard.
- Related: [Farrowdeep Mine](../../locations/farrowdeep-mine.md), an abandoned silver mine in the mountains northeast of Neverwinter.
- Details were generated or clarified during play.

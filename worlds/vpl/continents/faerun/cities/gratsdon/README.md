# Gratsdon

A town with a strong halfling culture, known for its annual harvest festival. Its location is not recorded.

## Overview

- In [session-2024-12-01-dm3](../../../../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm3.md), the town fell behind preparing the festival, which is crucial to its halfling culture. The wizard [Fangil](../../../../characters/npcs/fangil.md) asked the Amethyst Guard to help.
- Horse-sized birds called "gobble-lins" damage the local crops, and are tasty fare for festival patrons. They are the wild kind of the same [creature](../../../../creatures/gobble-lins.md) as the sentient Gobble-ins. Established by the owner after the sessions.

# Session 2024-12-01

Library game. The only source is a Discord post, an in-world bulletin to Amethyst Guard members dated 12/3/2024, so everything here is from Discord messages. The date 12/1/2024 is inferred, the Sunday before the post, and the session may have been on another day. The owner does not remember this session. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

At the request of the wizard [Fangil](../characters/npcs/fangil.md), the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) helped the town of [Gratsdon](../continents/faerun/cities/gratsdon/README.md) prepare its annual harvest festival. The festival is crucial to local halfling culture, and the town had fallen behind. The bulletin calls it "lighter fare."

## Party

Not recorded. The bulletin names no characters, only "Guard members."

## Events

1. The town needed the Guard to secure and prepare food, assemble and place decorations, and ensure entertainment was available.
2. The decorations and entertainment were simple.
3. Securing food meant dealing with a pack of "gobble-lins", large birds that destroy local crops but make tasty fare for festival patrons.

## Soul Shaker notice

The same bulletin warns Guard members that a second party met [the Godsbreath soul shaker](../characters/npcs/godsbreath-soul-shaker.md), a [soul shaker](../creatures/soul-shaker.md), outside the gates of Neverwinter. They confirmed it is the same creature met in [session-2024-11-17-dm3](session-2024-11-17-dm3.md). Further research found how to end the threat permanently: if a party meets it, they must destroy all of its hands before leaving. Who the second party was, and when that encounter happened, are not recorded.

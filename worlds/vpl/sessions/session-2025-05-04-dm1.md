# Session 2025-05-04

Library game. Adventure: recover a family heirloom for an old dragonborn before he dies.

## Premise

Ilxaxergesh Borquum, an old dragonborn, wanted to pass a family heirloom to his son before he dies, and hired the party to retrieve it from the old family house.

- [Ilxaxergesh Borquum](../characters/npcs/ilxaxergesh-borquum.md), father. Pronounced "Ill-zaxs-ergesh."
- [Preltann Borquum](../characters/npcs/preltann-borquum.md), son. Pronounced "Prel-tann." He does not appear in play.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md): fire genasi fighter
- [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md)
- [Issabella](../characters/pcs/issabella.md): high elf
- [Rowan Mossroot](../characters/pcs/rowan-mossroot.md): human druid
- [Olus](../characters/pcs/olus.md): dwarf cleric

## Events

1. The party went to the [Borquum house](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/borquum-house.md) in Neverwinter, met the servant and housekeeper [Mashid](../characters/npcs/mashid.md), and accepted the quest. The agreed pay is not recorded exactly, probably around 50 gp.
2. They were attacked by an assassin, the snake folk [Ishaxa](../characters/npcs/ishaxa.md), and her henchman, the dragonborn brute [Velkin Shavull](../characters/npcs/velkin-shavull.md).
3. Ember fist-bumped Rowan Mossroot, who was in bear form. The party knocked out Velkin and captured Ishaxa.
4. They took Ishaxa to [The Akward Canvas](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/akward-canvas.md), where [Leera](../characters/npcs/leera.md) is the proprietor, and found a contract on Ember's life there. The Guard calls the beings behind it the [demons from the Akward Canvas](../factions/demons-from-the-akward-canvas.md).
5. The contract probably came from a gnome the party called the "pink ranger," from a previous adventure. Ember had killed four of the gnome's friends in an adventure that is not recorded. The gnome is otherwise unimportant and has no file.
6. The party used an amulet to banish Ishaxa back to Infernus. The amulet's details are not recorded.
7. They left on good terms with Leera.
8. They went to the Borquum family's old house in a spooky, crumbled, abandoned village. The house was surprisingly intact. The village's location is not recorded.
9. Upstairs they fought animated armor and found one cat. They then fought shadows and a wooden construct gryphon, and found more cats.
10. They collected the [collector's case](../items/collectors-case.md) and the collector's trinkets, and brought them back to Ilxaxergesh. He was thrilled to have both and paid an extra 500 gp.

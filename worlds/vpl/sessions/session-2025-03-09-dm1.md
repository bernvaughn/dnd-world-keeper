# Session 2025-03-09

Library game. Adventure: end a string of burglaries in the gnome vacation town of [Lumenshire](../continents/faerun/cities/lumenshire/README.md). The date 3/9/2025 is inferred as the second Sunday of March. The only source is an in-world report posted to players on 3/12/2025, summarized below.

## Premise

The [Prismatic Gnome Rangers](../factions/prismatic-gnome-rangers.md) were burgling Lumenshire, a fancy vacation town southeast of Neverwinter.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md)
- [Issabella](../characters/pcs/issabella.md): spelled "Isabella" in the report
- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md): a fire genasi
- [Varis](../characters/pcs/varis.md)
- [Malachi](../characters/pcs/malachi.md)

## Events

1. The party set a trap by renting a vacation home and baiting the Rangers with valuables.
2. The [Green Ranger](../characters/npcs/green-ranger.md) took the bait and was captured after a brief scuffle.
3. That evening, the remaining Rangers, Pink, Yellow, Blue, and Red, ambushed the party to free their comrade.
4. Ember used the home's sauna as a strategic refuge. The heat was too much for the small gnomes, but she found comfort in it and used it to launch devastating attacks.
5. All the Rangers were defeated except the elusive [Pink Ranger](../characters/npcs/pink-ranger.md).
6. Lumenshire was safe again, and Ember's name now burns bright in the town's history.

## Aftermath

- The Pink Ranger is probably the gnome who later put a contract on Ember's life. See [session-2025-05-04-dm1](session-2025-05-04-dm1.md).
- The Green Ranger was held as a prisoner of the Amethyst Guard and escaped before trial. Established by the owner after the session. The report does not say what happened to him.

## Notes

- The report says the Rangers were "defeated." The 5/4 session says Ember killed four of the Pink gnome's friends. The Green Ranger escaped, so at most three of them can have died. The two accounts are kept as written, and the mismatch is not resolved.

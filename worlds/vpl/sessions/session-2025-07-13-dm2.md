# Session 2025-07-13

Library game. Adventure: rescue a merchant's missing relative. The only source is a Discord post, a report with no header, so everything here is from Discord messages. The date 7/13/2025 is the same as [session-2025-07-13-dm1](session-2025-07-13-dm1.md), with a different DM and party. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

A group of seven [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) members helped a merchant, [Olgar Greenthorn](../characters/npcs/olgar-greenthorn.md), rescue his missing cousin [Garvin Lorne](../characters/npcs/garvin-lorne.md).

## Party

Seven Guard members. Their names are not recorded.

## Events

1. The party went to a warehouse to retrieve a map that would reveal the location of the transport wagons.
2. There they had an extended fight with [David Davidson](../characters/npcs/david-davidson.md), whom the report calls "David Davidson Jr." The owner confirmed he is the same character. They defeated him, he revealed the information the party needed, and they begrudgingly released him, short one [Wand of Fireball](../items/wand-of-fireball.md).
3. They traveled to an old abandoned keep, [Daggerford Keep](../continents/faerun/locations/daggerford-keep.md) ("not THAT Daggerford Keep"), and found Garvin bloody and beaten.
4. Pressed, Garvin admitted it was his idea to steal the loot and make money for himself. He was deceived by [Serina the Shadowbinder](../characters/npcs/serina-the-shadowbinder.md), and betrayed once he was no longer needed.
5. The party returned Garvin to his uncle. Olgar was angry and hurt, but happy to have his cousin back relatively unharmed. He paid the group what he owed.

## Aftermath

- This marks the Guard's second failed job involving recovering stores of magical items, after the one in [session-2025-05-04-dm2](session-2025-05-04-dm2.md). Talk among the merchant class says there is growing hesitancy to hire the Guard when items of great value are involved.

## Notes

- The report calls Garvin Olgar's cousin, then says the party returned him to "his uncle." The owner is not sure which is right, so both are kept.

## Rewards

- Olgar paid what he owed. The amount is not recorded, and neither is XP.

# Session 2026-04-12

Library game. Adventure: recover a lost family heirloom lamp from a treasure vault. The adventure was made up by the DM. The reward loot was pre-rolled with a loot app.

## Premise

[Myenceth Rhozire](../characters/npcs/myenceth-rhozire.md), a copper dragonborn collector, was looking for adventurers to find the [Lost Lamp of Rhozire](../items/lost-lamp-of-rhozire.md), an heirloom owned by his ancestors.

The lead was [Urchin Goldrat](../characters/npcs/urchin-goldrat.md), a dwarf fisherman branded with a map on his chest. The map marks the location of a treasure hoard in the forest, and is incomprehensible until an observer says "I promise to take nothing that does not belong to me from the Goldrat Hoard." Urchin hangs out at the [Salty Shackle](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/salty-shackle.md), a fisherman bar.

Bandits had made camp at the [Goldrat Hoard](../continents/faerun/locations/goldrat-hoard.md). They thought the entrance was cool, but did not know how to unlock it. The instructions are very simple and written in Dwarven. Inside, a cavern tempts visitors to take things, and treasure golems attack anyone who does.

## Party

Members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Astrid Tealeaf](../characters/pcs/astrid.md)
- [Kronos](../characters/pcs/kronos.md)
- Other members were likely present but are not recorded.

## Events

1. [Astrid](../characters/pcs/astrid.md) upgraded her [rainbow rapier](../items/rainbow-rapier.md) to +1 for 1,000 gp. She also found 3 gp and a severed wood elf ear. Where is not recorded.
2. The party went to Urchin Goldrat at the Salty Shackle and bribed him with booze to come with them. He slept drunkenly through most of the adventure.
3. They went to the forest, northeast of Neverwinter, and were attacked by a barbed devil and a shambling mound.
4. They found an encampment with the entrance to a dwarf vault and killed the occupants with fireballs and other violence.
5. They went into the vault. [Kronos](../characters/pcs/kronos.md) greedily grabbed a 1,000 gp diamond, and the vault began to collapse. The party grabbed the lamp and sprinted out as the cave collapsed. Everyone escaped, but it was close.
6. They left the gladiator and bandit, who is the same person as Urchin Goldrat, to go on his way, and went back.
7. They returned the lamp to Myenceth, who paid them the coins from the reward.

## Loot

Pre-rolled reward:

- 4,000 gp: Myenceth paid the party in coins.
- Five 100 gp gemstones
- One bag of tricks, tan
- One ring of water walking

Whether the party received the gemstones, the bag of tricks, and the ring of water walking is not recorded, and neither is who took what. Kronos kept the 1,000 gp diamond.

## Ending

Success. The lamp was recovered and returned to Myenceth.

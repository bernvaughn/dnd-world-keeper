# Session 2026-02-05

Library game. Adventure: help a gnome wizard bring his lover back. The adventure was made up by the DM, with no outside source.

## Premise

[Fizzwidget Brambleloop](../characters/npcs/fizzwidget-brambleloop.md), a gnome wizard in Neverwinter, wanted to bring back [Grakka Skullcruncher](../characters/npcs/grakka-skullcruncher.md), his lover. She was a regular orc who died in battle and had her soul trapped in a cursed sword.

He was building a special iron golem that would take the soul from the sword and give Grakka a body again. He needed three more important items to finish it:

1. **Titanheart Core.** At the [Gravelgaze Shrine](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/gravelgaze-shrine.md), an earth elemental shrine. The party had to ruin the shrine to awaken its guardians, then extract their cores to craft the core.
2. **Breath Bellows.** At the [Cinderdeep Forge](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/cinderdeep-forge.md), an ancient dwarven forge inhabited by soot mephits, who keep stealing tools. The party needed tools to extract the bellows from the ruined magical forge. [Mordin](../characters/npcs/mordin.md) is associated with it.
3. **Binding Sigil Plate.** At [The Driftspire](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/the-driftspire.md), a ruined wizard tower with floating debris. Sigils there captured memories of Grakka while she destroyed the tower. The party had to recover them and form them into a sigil plate.

The final step was the Heartforge ritual, a skill challenge that decides how well it goes. A good ending has the Iron Orc Grakka living and embracing Fizzwidget. A bad ending has the sword corrupting, forcing everyone to run from the iron golem while Grakka's soul moves on to the afterlife.

## Party

Members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md). Which members took part is not recorded.

## Events

1. The party went to check on Fizzwidget, who was busy working on an iron golem. He offered 600 gp each: 300 gp up front and 300 gp on success.
2. They went to the Gravelgaze Shrine first and fought the earth elementals. They won after a tough fight. The elementals gave 3,000 sp, 200 gp, and two 100 gp gems.
3. They went to the Cinderdeep Forge, avoided the mephits (thinking they were imps), and got the big bellows.
4. They returned to Fizzwidget with the cores and bellows. He gave them some additional money and a couple of potions.
5. They went to The Driftspire, collected the plates, fought Grakka's spirit, and won. They got the [Thunderous Mace](../items/thunderous-mace.md).
6. They returned to Fizzwidget and gave him the rest.
7. The Heartforge ritual succeeded without much drama, and Grakka the Iron Orc was created, lover of Fizzwidget.

## Ending

Success. Grakka lives as an Iron Orc and embraces Fizzwidget. The Thunderous Mace was consumed by the golem.

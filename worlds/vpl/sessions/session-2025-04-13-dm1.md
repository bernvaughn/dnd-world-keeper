# Session 2025-04-13

Library game. Adventure: rescue a brewmaster and a recipe from a necromancer in the ruins of Blackstone. The date 4/13/2025 is inferred as the second Sunday of April. The only source is an in-world report posted to players on 4/15/2025, summarized below.

## Premise

[Mortimer Graves](../characters/npcs/mortimer-graves.md), a necromancer with dreams of tavernkeeping, had abducted [Turdrag Hardbelt](../characters/npcs/turdrag-hardbelt.md) to force him to brew Frosthaven Ale under duress, in the abandoned ruins of [Blackstone](../continents/faerun/locations/blackstone.md).

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md)
- [Rowan Mossroot](../characters/pcs/rowan-mossroot.md)
- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md)
- [Ziri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md)
- [Zephyr](../characters/pcs/zephyr.md)

## Events

1. The party fought Graves' skeletal army in the shadowed streets of Blackstone.
2. In a cave beneath the ruins, a hulking bone golem guarded the [Frosthaven Ale recipe](../items/frosthaven-ale-recipe.md).
3. Rowan sneaked close in rat form. Ember scorched the golem with burning hands. Zephyr delivered the final blow with an eldritch blast, shattering it.
4. Graves was killed.
5. The party rescued Turdrag, recovered the recipe, and claimed a hoard of artifacts.

## In-world commentary

The report quotes two NPCs, kept here as color:

- [Tavrin Hearthflame](../characters/npcs/tavrin-hearthflame.md), a historian of necromancers, called it "almost tragic": Graves pictured a lively pub where people gathered, unaware of its dark origins, and believed he could build a community even by bending others' will.
- [Brelthar Ironspigot](../characters/npcs/brelthar-ironspigot.md), head of the [Amber Tun Brewers' Guild](../factions/amber-tun-brewers-guild.md), said the party saved "a piece of history."

## Rewards

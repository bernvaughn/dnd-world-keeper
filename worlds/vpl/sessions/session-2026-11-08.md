# Session 2026-11-08

Library game. Adventure: "Flock of the Fallen Leaves". The adventure was made up by the DM. The party came across it on their way north to Neverwinter, and no one hired them.

## Premise

A wagon carrying food for a kitchen in Neverwinter, part of an ongoing deal, was being raided on the road south of Neverwinter. The raiders were the [Flock of the Fallen Leaves](../factions/flock-of-the-fallen-leaves.md), the Gobble-ins: former robbers turned into giant, sentient turkeys after they wished on a genie's lamp to be "powerful and ruthless goblins." They settled in the woods, then went back to banditry to protect their home. Eight Gobble-ins exist, and four took part in the wagon raids.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md)
- [Atlas](../characters/pcs/atlas.md)
- [Baldric](../characters/pcs/baldric.md)
- [Sir Doug](../characters/pcs/sir-doug.md)

Also present: [Tobin Rusk](../characters/npcs/tobin-rusk.md), a wagon guard from [Penny-Pincher Pike & Shield](../factions/penny-pincher-pike-and-shield.md), and [Mara Venn](../characters/npcs/mara-venn.md), the wagon driver. The wagon horses were Clover and Thistle.

## Events

1. The party found the wagon and its crew at the [Gobble-in estate](../continents/faerun/locations/gobble-in-estate.md), two modest farmhouses and a guest house. A snare trap was set on the path, and Tobin dodged it.
2. [Pip](../characters/npcs/pip.md), the lookout, was knocked unconscious by Atlas. [Gobb the Red](../characters/npcs/gobb-the-red.md) mistakenly thought Pip was dead. In retaliation, Gobb killed Tobin.
3. [Auntie Brindle](../characters/npcs/auntie-brindle.md), older and unarmed, came to help Pip.
4. [Cluckbane](../characters/npcs/cluckbane.md) fought the party and was killed by an arrow.
5. Baldric cast revivify on Tobin and on Cluckbane. The [cloak of displacement](../items/cloak-of-displacement.md) was surrendered to bring Cluckbane back. Tobin agreed to serve Baldric's family as payment.
6. Ember found a harmless magical echo of the transformation and a roster of the eight Gobble-ins, near the [cracked genie lamp](../items/cracked-genie-lamp.md), which had been tossed into a sinkhole.

## Resolution

- The party backed down. Gobb offered the flock's loot and an end to the raids.
- Cluckbane was tried by a judge in Neverwinter for robbery and assault. His sentence was restitution and a year of supervised road-warden service. The Amethyst Guard is unrelated to that service. Afterward, he hopes to join the Guard.
- Atlas wants to become Cluckbane's best friend.
- The lamp is connected to [Zhudee the Grand](../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md). The nature of the connection is not recorded.

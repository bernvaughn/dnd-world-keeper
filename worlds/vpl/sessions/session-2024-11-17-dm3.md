# Session 2024-11-17

Library game. The only source is a post in Discord, so everything here is from Discord messages. Adventure: find supplies and artisans in the town of Godsbreath to rebuild the guild keep. The post is dated 11/18/2024, and the owner placed the session on the Sunday before. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

The [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) was looking for resources and skilled artisans to rebuild the guild keep, which is the Guard's headquarters. The party journeyed upriver to [Godsbreath](../continents/faerun/cities/godsbreath/README.md), where there was word that adventurers' help would earn building supplies, food, and skilled carpenters.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Roland](../characters/pcs/roland.md)
- [Jax](../characters/pcs/jax.md)
- [Mithran](../characters/pcs/mithran.md)
- [Faelyn](../characters/pcs/faelyn.md)

## Events

1. The party enjoyed some local festivities in Godsbreath.
2. Farmers with solid red eyes attacked the town. [Auntie Deanna](../characters/npcs/auntie-deanna.md), the local leader, explained that this was why the town needed adventurers.
3. The party headed to the nearby farmland where the possessed townsfolk came from. On the way, they met [Kiarra](../characters/npcs/kiarra.md), Deanna's goddaughter, who was already investigating.
4. Kiarra told them of a creature from local folklore, a soul shaker, long dismissed as a story to frighten disobedient children.
5. Mithran searched the basement of an abandoned farmhouse and found disembodied hands digging for something. The soul shaker, an undead amalgamation of arms, was real.
6. In the fight, Roland fell. The party won.
7. The grateful townsfolk revived Roland and delivered the promised supplies. They withheld much of the monetary reward in exchange for the medical help.

## Rewards

- Building supplies and food, as promised. Amounts and the number of carpenters are not recorded.
- Most of the monetary reward was withheld for medical help. The amount is not recorded.

# Session 2024-11-17

Library game. The only source is a post in Discord, so everything here is from Discord messages. Adventure: rescue an architect from the Mentiri in the city of Dis, in Hell. The post is dated 11/18/2024, and the owner placed the session on the Sunday before. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

Following a lead and transportation from [Lord Pezz](../characters/npcs/lord-pezz.md), the party went to [Dis](../continents/hell/cities/dis/README.md), a smoldering, rusty city deep in Hell. The [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) was rebuilding after a big dragon attack that ravaged its headquarters.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Droon Toon](../characters/pcs/droon-toon.md): the post spells the name "Drountoun"
- [Kronos](../characters/pcs/kronos.md): the post says "Sir Kronos"
- [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md)

## Events

1. Lord Pezz gave the party a lead and transportation to Dis.
2. They avoided the dangers, traps, and temptations of Hell.
3. They broke into [the Mentiri](../continents/hell/cities/dis/locations/the-mentiri.md) and rescued a mysterious architect named [Labor](../characters/npcs/labor.md).
4. They waited for Lord Pezz in [Sigil's Sister](../continents/hell/locations/sigils-sister.md), a magical hotel.
5. They regrouped and returned to Neverwinter, hoping to rebuild the Amethyst Guard "bigger and better than ever."

# Session 2024-12-01

Library game. The only source is a Discord post, an in-world report dated 12/7/2024, so everything here is from Discord messages. The date 12/1/2024 is inferred. VPL sessions are on Sundays, so it is the Sunday before the post, the same day as [session-2024-12-01-dm3](session-2024-12-01-dm3.md) with a different party. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

Four guild members were returning to Neverwinter from a successful job when they came to a maze. The job is not recorded. The report says "the guild," taken here as the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

## Events

1. A man standing outside [the maze](../continents/faerun/locations/the-maze.md) asked the group if they would pay to enter.
2. He had them sign a waiver saying any injuries in the maze were not the fault of the maze or its staff.
3. The group signed and paid the fee.
4. On entering, they were attacked by bean stalks.
5. As written in the report, only two members escaped with their lives, and the survivors brought back the bodies of the fallen so they could be properly mourned.

## Ruling by the owner

The owner does not remember which characters died, so ruled retroactively that all four barely escaped with their lives. The report's account of two deaths and recovered bodies is kept above as written, and this ruling takes precedence.

# Session 2025-09-14

Library game. Adventure: a djinni has taken over one of Neverwinter's great libraries. The party escaped, but the adventure is technically unfinished.

## Premise

[Zhudee the Grand](../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md), a djinni, took over the [Freedom Athenaeum](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/freedom-athenaeum.md) and turned it into a luxury spa. He claimed it because [Beram Massan](../characters/npcs/beram-massan.md), a recent hire to the library's senior board of librarians, lost Zhudee's prized book, [The Creation of the Void](../items/the-creation-of-the-void.md). The book was a trap, meant to put the library in debt to Zhudee. Beram was in hot water for losing it and for angering him.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [CLAM](../characters/pcs/clam.md): Warforged Monk
- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md): Fire Genasi Fighter
- [Brute](../characters/pcs/brute.md): Warforged Barbarian
- [Aramai](../characters/pcs/aramai.md): Half-Elf Paladin
- [Oggy](../characters/pcs/oggy.md): Elf Fighter

## Events

1. The party found Beram, talked to him, and learned that a djinni had taken over the library.
2. They paid a water elemental greeter, who is unnamed.
3. They went up to a platform overlooking an island above a swirling whirlpool. The island is inside the building, a planar trick.
4. They met an aarakocra, an unrelated NPC, had a nice conversation, and learned a little about the owner of the spa.
5. They found [Tondin Hallowflower](../characters/npcs/tondin-hallowflower.md), an alchemist with a meeting scheduled with the djinni, and followed him to it. They helped with his sales deal to supply aromatics for the spa through [Hallowflower Aromatics](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/hallowflower-aromatics.md), by negotiating and giving him moral support.
6. They stayed and made deals with Zhudee.
7. [Ember](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) and [Oggy](../characters/pcs/oggy.md) made no deals and had to escape. They took some hits from water elementals but dodged their way out. Oggy was knocked out while escaping, and [Brute](../characters/pcs/brute.md) carried him out.

## The deals

- **Aramai and Brute:** they agreed to provide Zhudee another business to add to his domain, as an entrance to the spa. In exchange, he would reopen the library as part of his business.
- **CLAM:** a sneaky deal to secure a free exit. Zhudee was amused and let him leave with a grain of sand, in exchange for 4 peppers from CLAM.
- **Ember and Oggy:** no deals.

## Ending

The party escaped but technically failed. Brute is left to fulfill his side of the deal with Zhudee. That makes an open thread and a possible future adventure.

## Open threads

- Brute still owes Zhudee another business to add to his domain as an entrance to the spa. Until then, the library stays closed.
- The Freedom Athenaeum's grand reading rooms may reopen as a library and spa fusion, with a planned Library Lounge, if the Guard delivers.

# Session 2025-05-04

Library game. The only source is a Discord post, an in-world memo to Amethyst Guard members signed [Lord Pezz](../characters/npcs/lord-pezz.md), dated 5/6/2025. The date 5/4/2025 is inferred: it is the Sunday before the post, a parallel session to [session-2025-05-04-dm1](session-2025-05-04-dm1.md) with a different party. Everything here is from Discord messages. The DM ended the memo with a note that the spellings of the characters' names need a double-check, so every name below is unconfirmed. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## What happened

The memo does not describe the adventure itself. It says only that a highly sensitive task given to the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) by the city failed, and that magic items were lost. The incident, the task, and the items are not recorded.

## Party

Not fully recorded. The memo names these as involved:

- [Ziri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md)
- [Varis](../characters/pcs/varis.md)
- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md)
- [Mancler](../characters/pcs/mancler.md)
- [David Davidson](../characters/npcs/david-davidson.md): turned on the Guard

## Events, as the memo states them

1. After negotiation with [Auril's Grin](../factions/aurils-grin.md), Ziri, Varis, Strange, and Mancler were safely returned to the guild.
2. David Davidson is no longer affiliated with the Guard and is considered hostile and dangerous. The memo calls the Guard "The Reborn Amethyst Guard." The owner decided the official name stays "Amethyst Guard."
3. [Lord Neverwinter](../characters/npcs/lord-neverwinter.md) and high-ranking city officials said publicly that they were disappointed in the quality of care given to the task. The Guard promised the city to be on the front line offering assistance, and that recovering the lost magic items is of utmost importance.

## Guard policy changes

- Fire, "magic or otherwise," is "highly discouraged," and senior leadership is discussing whether it warrants an outright ban.
- The Guard will begin offering workshops on cooperation and teamwork, since mercenary work can be chaotic and high stress.
- The memo ends with a reminder that members represent the Guard, not only themselves.

# Session 2026-09-13

Library game. Adventure: a librarian vanishes, and the party must keep an island library running for its devil patrons. The adventure was made up by the DM, loosely based on the video game *Monk Took Book*.

## Premise

The librarian of [Candlemere Stables](../continents/faerun/locations/candlemere-stables.md), an island library near Neverwinter, mysteriously disappeared. The party had to investigate and fulfill the library's contracts with devils by checking out books to them until a new librarian arrived.

The devils came from a scholarly college in Infernus, the [Collegium Infernalis](../factions/collegium-infernalis.md). Presumably its headmaster, [Vessarith Ashquill](../characters/npcs/vessarith-ashquill.md), holds the contract. The librarian is [Maelis Quillmere](../characters/npcs/maelis-quillmere.md). Details of the contract, the college, and the librarian were generated after the session and approved by the owner.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Pipwick](../characters/pcs/pipwick.md): squirrel Warlock
- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md): Fire Genasi Fighter
- [Asha](../characters/pcs/asha.md): Dragonborn Rogue
- [Droon Toon](../characters/pcs/droon-toon.md): Human Cleric
- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md): Tiefling Wizard, still a Hallowed One

## Events

1. The party took a tortle pleasure cruise boat, [Tide and Tardy](../ships/tide-and-tardy.md), to the island library.
2. They went in and had to get books for the devils.
3. They killed monsters and served the books to the devils.
4. A ghost gorgon had started attacking the library. It petrified [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md) and the librarian. The party defeated it.
5. They unpetrified Strange and the librarian, and saved the day.

## Ending

Success. The librarian was able to resume library operations.

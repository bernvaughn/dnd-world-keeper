# Session 2024-10-13

Library game. Adventure: secure two ships for the Amethyst Guard.

## Premise

[Mulgric Hardbelt](../characters/npcs/mulgric-hardbelt.md), a quest giver at the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md), hired four brave members of the Guard.

## Party

- [Nebula Autumnson](../characters/pcs/nebula-autumnson.md): the clever rogue
- [Lord Eerek](../characters/pcs/lord-eerek.md): the noble aarakocra
- [Lady Arka](../characters/pcs/lady-arka.md): the brave and drunk dwarven fighter
- [Headless](../characters/pcs/headless.md): the steadfast human cleric

## Events

1. The party went to find the [Cotswald](../ships/cotswald.md), a ship being squatted in by [Golric](../characters/npcs/golric.md), at the [Neverwinter docks](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/docks.md).
2. They got the ship and scared Golric away.
3. They hired 19 sailors. 3 died in battle, and the other 16 were recruited into the Amethyst Guard. None were named.
4. They fought the goblin ship, the stolen [Acacia](../ships/the-acacia.md), led by Captain Cruelheart, an undead minotaur.
5. They won, barely.
6. They secured the Cotswald (a sloop) and The Acacia (a galleon) for the Amethyst Guard.

## Loot

- A bunch of gold pieces (amount not recorded)
- [Axe of Sacrifice](../items/axe-of-sacrifice.md), which Lady Arka took
- A bunch of [potions of curing petrification](../items/potions-of-curing-petrification.md), which were needed to save Headless

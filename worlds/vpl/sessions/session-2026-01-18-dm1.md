# Session 2026-01-18

Library game. Adventure: find out why a toll road is not reporting its numbers correctly, and secure the fort behind it for Borquum Holdings.

## Premise

[Borquum Holdings](../factions/borquum-holdings.md) noticed that a toll road had not been reporting its numbers correctly, so the party needed to check it out. Who hired the party is not recorded.

## Events

1. The party traveled from Neverwinter to [Castle Coinfall](../continents/faerun/locations/castle-coinfall.md), about a day's travel away, and rested until morning.
2. They approached the toll booth and found a dead dwarf inside, who exploded. They took the toll money, some silver pieces. The amount is not recorded.
3. They approached the castle and went in. An illusion of a different dwarf, unnamed, tried to get them to pull a lever. The lever flooded the courtyard with water. Whether the party pulled it is not recorded.
4. The castle was full of traps. The party disarmed them and ventured in.
5. They fought a mimic and a drider. The drider had set all the traps, because it wanted the castle. It was not named.
6. They later learned that the dwarf in the toll booth had been violently exploded when the drider attacked him.
7. They killed the enemies, claimed the loot, and secured the fort for Borquum Holdings.

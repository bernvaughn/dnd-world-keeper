# Session 2025-06-08

Library game. The only source is a Discord post in two parts, an in-world report titled "Guard Members Face Threats on the Open Sea," dated 6/19/2025. The date 6/8/2025 is inferred as the second Sunday of June. Everything here is from Discord messages. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

After a night out in the port of Neverwinter, four [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) members were roped into crewing [Captain Steve](../characters/npcs/captain-steve.md)'s fishing vessel. It was a way for their drinking companion to pay a debt to the captain. The companion is not named or recorded.

## Party

All members of the Amethyst Guard.

- [Astrid](../characters/pcs/astrid.md)
- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md)
- [Srorthen](../characters/pcs/srorthen.md)
- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md)

## Events

1. Before embarking the next day, Srorthen hired a local dockworker named David to join them. He is not linked to [David Davidson](../characters/npcs/david-davidson.md).
2. On the voyage, Captain Steve lamented the increased fees and costs the fishing fleet now faces, which force captains to fish farther into dangerous waters for valuable catches.
3. A group of sahuagin mercenaries attacked the ship and killed David. The party fought them off.
4. After rough seas on the way to Steve's fishing grounds, the crew set out lines and traps.
5. Late in the day they reeled in the day's trophy catch, an aboleth. Despite its mighty attack and several injured crew, they defeated it. It coughed up an [arcane gem of great power](../items/arcane-gem-of-great-power.md).
6. Back in port, David's wife and son were told stories of David's heroism, and Captain Steve gave them a large share of the catch's value.
7. The crew spotted the villain behind the increased fees at port, the familiar face of [Nolan](../characters/npcs/nolan.md), a warlock in service of the devil prince [Mammon](../characters/npcs/mammon.md).
8. Before leaving port, Strange used the recently acquired gem to make an "appropriately disrespectful gesture" at Nolan.

## Rewards

- The arcane gem. A large share of the catch's value went to David's family. Other rewards and XP are not recorded.

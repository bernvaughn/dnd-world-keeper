# Session 2025-08-03

Library game. Adventure: retrieve a tea set for [Ilxaxergesh Borquum](../characters/npcs/ilxaxergesh-borquum.md). The mission failed, and three of the four party members died.

## Premise

[Ilxaxergesh Borquum](../characters/npcs/ilxaxergesh-borquum.md) wanted the party to retrieve a [tea set](../items/ilxaxergesh-tea-set.md). The cups are shaped like his head and the teapot is shaped like a bronze dragon. The set is very exquisite but also tacky.

There was no pay. The party hired [Bring-Your-Own-Ship Cruises](../factions/bring-your-own-ship-cruises.md), a tortle charter company that Ilxaxergesh works with a lot, and put the cost on his tab.

## Party

Members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Zuri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md): the same character as Ziri from [session-2025-01-12-dm1](session-2025-01-12-dm1.md). The spelling is unconfirmed.
- [Astrid](../characters/pcs/astrid.md)
- [Olus](../characters/pcs/olus.md)
- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md)

## Events

1. **The Old Grape Pharmacy.** Zuri and Astrid robbed [The Old Grape Pharmacy](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/old-grape-pharmacy.md), breaking into its locked back room. Olus watched from a distance. Strange met the love of his life at a tavern, a safe distance away. This was a one-off scene. Astrid made it out with 3 stolen antidote pills and kept them.
2. **The arrest.** Zuri arrested Astrid as a member of the Amethyst Guard. The arrest was staged.
3. **The party ship.** The party met up at [Slow and Steady](../ships/slow-and-steady.md) and enjoyed the party ship, with hors d'oeuvres and an open bar. They then went over to a boat.
4. **The deserted ship.** They found a deserted ship, [The Cold Cup](../ships/the-cold-cup.md). Zuri grabbed the teapot, which awakened monsters made of bone, ship, and dead sailors. The ship itself was cursed, and the tea set also carried a curse, which the party learned about in play.
5. **The fight.** They fought skeletons, a bone and flesh golem, and a minotaur skeleton. The skeletons killed all the tortles. The party almost prevailed, but everyone was knocked out.

## Ending

- Zuri, Strange, and Olus died. Zuri and Olus stayed dead. Strange was later resurrected as a Hallowed One.
- Astrid made it to a deserted island, was rescued by a merchant ship, and returned to Neverwinter.
- The party failed to retrieve the treasure and the sentimental bronze dragon teapot.
- Both ships are presumed lost at sea.
- As far as Ilxaxergesh knows, the ship was lost and the party was defeated.

- **"Tragedy on the High Seas: Amethyst Guard's Quest Ends in Ruin," by Elysia Thorne.** It matches the notes above on the tea set, the Bring-Your-Own-Ship cruise on a tortle-run vessel, Zuri lifting the teapot, the undead, the tortle crew dying, three Guard members falling, and Astrid washing ashore on a deserted isle and being rescued by a passing merchant vessel. It adds that the party's ship was a pleasure vessel, that the deserted ship was presumed to be an abandoned pleasure ship, and that some undead were "stitched from bone and flesh" and others were reanimated sailors "fused with the ship itself." It calls Zuri the Guard captain and says the tea set, the pleasure cruiser, and the bodies of the fallen are presumed lost beneath the waves.

Zuri is said to have intercepted an attempted break-in at [The Old Grape Pharmacy](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/old-grape-pharmacy.md) the night before, spotted a person slipping "high-potency antidote pills" into their cloak, and arrested the unnamed suspect. The suspect is [Astrid](../characters/pcs/astrid.md), and the pills are the 3 she kept. It calls this Zuri's final recorded arrest before she was presumed lost at sea.
- The reports use "she" and "her" for [Zuri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md). The owner confirmed she was promoted to Guard captain, and when is not recorded.
- The in-world reporters, Elysia Thorne and Kaden Rose, have no files.

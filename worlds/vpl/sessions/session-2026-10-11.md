# Session 2026-10-11

Library game. Adventure: the apples in [Bramblewick](../continents/faerun/cities/bramblewick/README.md) rot during the apple festival, and the party tracks down why. The adventure was made up by the DM. Some details were generated or clarified during play.

## Premise

At Bramblewick's apple festival, things turned to chaos as the apples, and all food made with them, rotted. The party learned that the mayor had botched paying the witch [Mara Bramble](../characters/npcs/mara-bramble.md) for healing the town's orchards. She withdrew her blessing when the balance went unpaid.

## Party

All members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).

- [Lord Eerek](../characters/pcs/lord-eerek.md)
- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md): still a Hallowed One
- [Kronos](../characters/pcs/kronos.md): known officially as Sir Kronos

## Events

1. The party enjoyed the festival, then chaos broke out with rotten apples.
2. They found out the mayor had botched paying Mara Bramble for the tree healing. The contract's balance was never paid.
3. They traveled toward Mara's house and fought a treant and awakened trees, rot-touched animated trees near the orchard. They won. Strange was hurt but was healed.
4. At Mara's house, she gave Strange cookies and told her side of the contract story.
5. They went back to town and threw the mayor in jail. The party had learned the mayor was not really Alden Whitlock: [Tilda Brasspick](../characters/npcs/tilda-brasspick.md), a miner, had killed Alden and taken his place after his father Edric died.

## The contract

- Total fee: 20,000 gp. 10,000 gp was paid up front, and 10,000 gp was due at the first picking of sound fruit.
- The contract allowed Mara to withdraw her blessing if the balance was not paid. She did, and all the apples rotted.

## Ending

Tilda was jailed. [Sella Vane](../characters/npcs/sella-vane.md) stayed as acting mayor. The apples never recovered.

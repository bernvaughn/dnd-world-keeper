# Session 2024-11-24

Library game. A standalone adventure: exploring a temple and finding a cult, the [Dark Embrace](../factions/dark-embrace.md), trying to raise its god.

The date was corrected to 11/24/2024. The notes are dated "11/24," which was first misread as November 1. A Discord report posted 11/27/2024 confirmed the later date.

## Party

- [Roland](../characters/pcs/roland.md)
- [Maze](../characters/pcs/maze.md)
- [Moress](../characters/pcs/moress.md)
- [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md): spelled "Egilflametap" in this session's notes

## Events

1. The party went to the temple entrance and took a rest.
2. They found the camp of [Amaakos](../characters/npcs/amaakos.md), but he was not there.
3. They woke up the next morning and went into the temple.
4. They attacked the snakes in the pit room, which held lots of oil and vines.
5. They defeated most of the guards in the pit room.
6. [Roland](../characters/pcs/roland.md) was captured. [Maze](../characters/pcs/maze.md), [Moress](../characters/pcs/moress.md), and [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md) escaped back to Neverwinter and recovered at the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md).
7. Roland escaped with Amaakos after being captured, and reported back that a cult is trying to raise its god. The party will monitor the situation for now.

## From Discord messages

An in-world report written to be posted to the players, titled "Dungeon Explorer Rescues Hapless Merchant" and dated 11/27/2024. It is summarized here, not kept verbatim.

- [Amaakos](../characters/npcs/amaakos.md) is described as a famed spelunker returning from another expedition. It took him to an ancient subterranean temple of a long-forgotten serpent deity, where ancient serpent folk awakened and trapped him.
- He heard a scuffle and saw members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) attack the serpent folk head-on to clear him an escape route.
- During his escape he rescued [Roland](../characters/pcs/roland.md), whom the report calls a beloved merchant, and both made it back to town safely. Roland retired to become a merchant after this adventure.
- Amaakos thanks [Maze](../characters/pcs/maze.md), [Moress](../characters/pcs/moress.md), and "Egilflametap" for enabling his escape. That is the spelling variant already recorded for [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md).
- It does not conflict with the notes above. It adds the serpent deity, the serpent folk, and Amaakos's side of the escape.

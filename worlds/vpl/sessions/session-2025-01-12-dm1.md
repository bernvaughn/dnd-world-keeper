# Session 2025-01-12

Library game. A one-shot heist, run from the published adventure "Prisoner 13" in *Keys from the Golden Vault*. The adventure may be run again in the future.

## Premise

The party, members of the [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md), went undercover to [Revel's End](../continents/faerun/locations/revels-end.md), an island prison near Neverwinter, to try to break out a prisoner. They went in on the staff boat with cover jobs.

## Party

- [Chloe Amastacia](../characters/pcs/chloe-amastacia.md): cover job as a chef
- [Gerard Esoog](../characters/pcs/gerard-esoog.md): cover job as a chef
- [Ziri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md): cover job as a guard
- [Alerin](../characters/pcs/alerin.md): cover job as a guard
- [Bryn Aeler Sr.](../characters/pcs/bryn-aeler-sr.md): cover job as a guard

## Events

1. The party used the staff boat to get to Revel's End.
2. There were 21 prisoners at the prison.
3. They talked to prisoner 13 and made a deal: they would fetch the list of crimes and related papers from the office, in exchange for help getting the key to the vault. They did not yet know what the key is.
4. They went up to the absolution chamber and the warden's office and talked to the warden.
5. The conversation turned into a battle. The guard captain died, and the warden teleported away with Dimension Door and raised a high alert.
6. The attempt to break out the prisoner failed.

## Ending

The session ended on a cliffhanger and the adventure was never finished. The party's return to the Amethyst Guard was handwaved so the next sessions could continue with something else.

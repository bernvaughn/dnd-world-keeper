## The break-ins

3. [The Akward Canvas](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/akward-canvas.md), run by [Leera](../characters/npcs/leera.md): the third

## Events

1. [Brimstone Whisk](../characters/npcs/brimstone-whisk.md), the trash pickup man, had been killed by the goblins, and his customers' information was stolen, which led to the break-ins. The party found him dead in his office in Neverwinter, alerted the guard, and did the proper paperwork.
2. The party worked with the Town Guard to investigate.
3. They killed the goblins, went to a bar, and finished off the rest of them there.
4. All the goblin burglars are dead.

# Session 2025-03-02

Library game. The only source is a Discord post, an in-world bulletin titled "Guard Betrayed," dated 3/8/2025. The date 3/2/2025 is inferred: the second Sunday of March was 3/9, after the post, so this is the Sunday before it, and the session may have been on another day such as 2/9/2025. Everything here is from Discord messages. The DM was not the owner of this repo. See `AGENTS.md` on DM codes.

## Premise

The [Amethyst Guard](../factions/amethyst-guard.md) was called to the town of [Smelton](../continents/faerun/cities/smelton/README.md) to help protect the local mine, after equipment was destroyed and workers were run off. [Nolan](../characters/npcs/nolan.md), the head of the town, warned that all previous attempts to deal with the threat had ended with no one returning.

## Party

Not recorded. The bulletin names no characters, only "the brave adventurers."

## Events

1. The party noticed something off in the town and approached the mine with caution.
2. They spoke with [Petro](../characters/npcs/petro.md), the goliath who had been attacking the mine.
3. They learned that Nolan had been corrupting the land and exploiting the townspeople.
4. They made a plan to run Nolan out of town.
5. After he left, the Guard distributed his wealth to the exploited miners.

## Bulletin warning

The bulletin ends by encouraging Guard members to use discretion when accepting jobs, and to make sure they are not deceived into aiding the cause of evil.

- Not recorded. Nolan's wealth went to the miners.

# Session 2025-11-09

Adventure: help the blacksmith shop Adore Ore craft a new batch of magic weapons.

## Premise

[Nylit](../characters/npcs/nylit.md), owner of [Adore Ore](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/adore-ore.md), needs help crafting a new batch of magic weapons. The party was offered 2 +1 weapons each, of their choice. Three problems stood in the way:

1. A shipment of [Blitzsteel ingots](../items/blitzsteel-ingots.md) was held up at the docks on the Eternal Emissary. [Captain Orion Darkwater](../characters/npcs/orion-darkwater.md) was at a standstill with Harbor Master [Karenaise](../characters/npcs/karenaise.md), and a docking arrangement had to be negotiated.
2. [Demonic blood](../items/demonic-blood.md) was needed, available relatively easily at [The Akward Canvas](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/akward-canvas.md) from [Leera](../characters/npcs/leera.md).
3. Minotaur leather was needed, through a deal with the nearby [Blood Orc's Fist](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/blood-orcs-fist.md) arena. The [Tribe of the Last Charge](../factions/tribe-of-the-last-charge.md) sends volunteers who want to die in combat and be made into weapons. The most recent seven had become the reigning champions and needed to be defeated.

## Party

- [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md): Fire Genasi Fighter
- [Clovis](../characters/pcs/clovis.md): Human Artificer
- [Droon Toon](../characters/pcs/droon-toon.md): Human (raised by dwarves) Cleric
- [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md): Tiefling Wizard
- [Asha](../characters/pcs/asha.md): Dragonborn Rogue

## Events

1. **Blood Orc's Fist (first visit).** The party was paid 500 gp to promote the fight and draw a crowd that night, and signed up to fight the minotaurs.
2. **Docks.** [Droon Toon](../characters/pcs/droon-toon.md) paid a 20 gp deposit, with 380 gp due after delivery, for 15 lbs of [adamantium](../items/adamantium.md) from the ship's dwarves. The party talked Karenaise down to reasonable dock fees, and the dwarves paid and delivered the ingots.
3. **The Akward Canvas.** [Ember](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) punched Leera in the throat and took the demonic blood. [Clovis](../characters/pcs/clovis.md) intimidated Leera into not telling anyone, drawing on his scary pirate background. Ember sold the blood to Nylit at 150 gp each, probably around 3 vials.
4. **Adore Ore.** The party delivered the materials. Nylit paid for the demonic blood, and the ingots were delivered per contract.
5. **Blood Orc's Fist (the fight).** Clovis bet 1500 gp and Ember bet 100 gp on the party winning, paid out at 2:1. The party defeated the minotaurs after a hard battle. [Rattarack](../characters/npcs/rattarack.md) was the biggest of the seven and the only one named.
6. **Adore Ore (finish).** Nylit made the [batch of magic weapons](../items/adore-ore-magic-weapons.md), and each player received a weapon. Droon Toon paid the remaining 380 gp and received the 15 lbs of adamantium.

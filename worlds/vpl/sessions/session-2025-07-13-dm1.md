# Session 2025-07-13

Library game. Adventure: escape the Mad Hatter's dinner party. The adventure was made up by the DM, built around a carousing table. The table's source is not recorded.

## Premise

[Carpus Catfig](../characters/npcs/carpus-catfig.md), a Mad Hatter figure, captured the party at his exclusive dinner party in Neverwinter, and they had to escape. Every character wore an ankle bracelet that teleported them back to his table when the next meal was served.

How the party was forced to attend, and the banquet's exact location, are not recorded.

## How it works

- At each meal, the dishes were determined by rolling a d100 two or three times. Each player chose which dish to eat from, and the dish decided where they ended up.
- The party then had to deal with whatever they found there and make progress toward escaping before the next meal.

## Rolls

### First round

- **72:** cake in the shape of Willow, an alley, and a tattoo of Willow. Taken by Genji, who went into a tavern, drank a drink, and succeeded on a Constitution save.
- **73:** decadent chocolate cake, an alley with a dead body. Taken by Willow, who woke up, looted the corpse, entered the tavern, and succeeded on a Constitution save.
- **71:** cake in the shape of Carpus, an alley, and a tattoo of Carpus Catfig. Taken by Ember, who woke up and entered the tavern but did not drink.

### Second round

- **14:** delicious beef ribs, waking up in a bed made of rats. Taken by Genji and Willow.
- **54:** the dish is not recorded, waking up in a temple at your own funeral. Taken by Rowan.
- **84:** chocolate chip cookies, waking up in an orphanage surrounded by orphans who blame you for eating all the cookies. Taken by Astrid and Egil.

### Third round

- **57:** half-moon cookies, a temple for a church service. Taken by Rowan and Willow.
- **70:** fried spiral potatoes, an alley with urchins who want to take your gold and sell your body parts. No one picked this.
- **4:** steak and eggs, waking up in jail with a cellmate who is an enemy. No one picked this.

### Fourth round

Ember was so resistant to eating any dishes that she broke free and attacked. It became a fight against two polar bears and Carpus Catfig.

## Ending

The party won the fight. Ember killed Carpus.

# Collegium Infernalis

A scholarly college in Infernus. Its devils borrow books from [Candlemere Stables](../continents/faerun/locations/candlemere-stables.md) under a contract.

## Leadership

- [Vessarith Ashquill](../characters/npcs/vessarith-ashquill.md): bone devil and headmaster

## The contract

Generated after the session and approved by the owner:

- The library lends the college's devils any book they request while it stands.
- If the librarian's post is empty, someone must staff the library until a new librarian arrives.
- A lapse would forfeit the island library to the college.

## Notes

- In [session-2026-09-13-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-09-13-dm1.md), the party checked out books to the college's devils while the librarian was petrified.
- The college's location within Infernus and the names of its other members are not recorded.

# Tribe of the Last Charge

A tribe of minotaurs. The name was chosen after the tribe's first appearance in play.

## Tradition

Members who want to die in combat and be made into weapons are sent to the [Blood Orc's Fist](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/blood-orcs-fist.md) arena in Neverwinter. This is an honorary practice in their culture. The arena supplies minotaur leather to [Adore Ore](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/adore-ore.md) through a deal.

## Known members

- [Rattarack](../characters/npcs/rattarack.md): the biggest of the seven most recent volunteers
- Six other minotaurs, unnamed

## History

- The seven most recent volunteers became the reigning champions of the arena. The party defeated them in [session-2025-11-09](../sessions/session-2025-11-09.md).

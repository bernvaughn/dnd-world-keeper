# Dark Embrace

A cult based in the temple [Szessith's Embrace](../continents/faerun/locations/szessiths-embrace.md). It worships Szessith the Coiled Dark, a long-forgotten serpent deity, and was trying to raise its god. Generated after the session and approved by the owner. The cult was never fleshed out otherwise.

## Notes

- Serpent folk awakened in the temple and trapped [Amaakos](../characters/npcs/amaakos.md). Whether they are members or servants is not recorded.
- In [session-2024-11-24-dm1](../sessions/session-2024-11-24-dm1.md), the cult captured [Roland](../characters/pcs/roland.md). He escaped with Amaakos and reported that the cult is trying to raise its god.
- The Amethyst Guard monitored the situation after that session. Whether the cult succeeded is not recorded.

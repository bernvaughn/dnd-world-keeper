# Demons from the Akward Canvas

The Amethyst Guard's name for the demons connected to [The Akward Canvas](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/akward-canvas.md). Their real name, if any, is not recorded.

## Notes

- [Leera](../characters/npcs/leera.md), the shop's proprietor, serves as a willing front that lets the demons enter this plane and kill people. He is not directly tied to the killings.
- The shop held a contract on [Ember Flamethrower](../characters/pcs/ember-flamethrower.md) in [session-2025-05-04-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm1.md).
- The in-world report for [session-2025-05-04-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm1.md) calls them "a secretive guild" fronted by Leera, and spells the shop "Awkward," a common typo. The owner confirmed it is the same group.
- Earlier, in [session-2025-11-09-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-11-09-dm1.md), the party obtained [demonic blood](../items/demonic-blood.md) at the shop.

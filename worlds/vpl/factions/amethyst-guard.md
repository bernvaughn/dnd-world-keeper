## Notes

- An in-world report on [session-2024-10-13-dm1](../sessions/session-2024-10-13-dm1.md) ends with "For the Amethyst Guard, by your side." It is that report's sign-off. Whether it is a real motto is not recorded.
- Where the Guard is based is not recorded.
- In [session-2024-11-24-dm1](../sessions/session-2024-11-24-dm1.md), [Maze](../characters/pcs/maze.md), [Moress](../characters/pcs/moress.md), and [Egil Flametrap](../characters/pcs/egil-flametrap.md) recovered here after escaping a temple back to Neverwinter.
- In [session-2024-12-08-dm1](../sessions/session-2024-12-08-dm1.md), five members worked with the Neverwinter Town Guard to stop a string of shop break-ins.
- In [session-2025-01-12-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-01-12-dm1.md), five members went undercover to [Revel's End](../continents/faerun/locations/revels-end.md) to break out a prisoner, and failed. The adventure ended on a cliffhanger, and their return to the Guard was handwaved.
- In [session-2025-05-04-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-05-04-dm1.md), five members recovered a family heirloom for an old dragonborn in Neverwinter, and uncovered a contract on one of their own at The Akward Canvas.
- In [session-2025-07-13-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-07-13-dm1.md), six members were captured at [Carpus Catfig](../characters/npcs/carpus-catfig.md)'s dinner party in Neverwinter and escaped by killing him.
- In [session-2025-08-03-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-08-03-dm1.md), four members sailed to retrieve a cursed tea set. [Ziri](../characters/pcs/ziri.md) and [Olus](../characters/pcs/olus.md) died for good, and [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md) died and was later resurrected as a Hallowed One. [Astrid](../characters/pcs/astrid.md) was the sole survivor.
- In [session-2025-09-14-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-09-14-dm1.md), five members went to the [Freedom Athenaeum](../continents/faerun/cities/neverwinter/locations/freedom-athenaeum.md) after a djinni turned it into a spa. They escaped, but [Brute](../characters/pcs/brute.md) still owes [Zhudee the Grand](../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md) another business, an open thread. Guard members get perks at the spa.
- In [session-2026-01-18-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-01-18-dm1.md), five members investigated a toll road for [Borquum Holdings](borquum-holdings.md) and secured [Castle Coinfall](../continents/faerun/locations/castle-coinfall.md) for it.
- In [session-2026-02-05-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-02-05-dm1.md), members helped a gnome wizard, [Fizzwidget Brambleloop](../characters/npcs/fizzwidget-brambleloop.md), gather three components for an iron golem that brought his lover back as an Iron Orc. Which members took part is not recorded.
- In [session-2026-04-12-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-04-12-dm1.md), members including [Astrid Tealeaf](../characters/pcs/astrid.md) and [Kronos](../characters/pcs/kronos.md) recovered the Lost Lamp of Rhozire from the [Goldrat Hoard](../continents/faerun/locations/goldrat-hoard.md) for the collector [Myenceth Rhozire](../characters/npcs/myenceth-rhozire.md).
- In [session-2026-09-13-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-09-13-dm1.md), five members kept the island library [Candlemere Stables](../continents/faerun/locations/candlemere-stables.md) running for devil patrons from the [Collegium Infernalis](collegium-infernalis.md) after its librarian vanished, and defeated a ghost gorgon.
- In [session-2026-10-11-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-10-11-dm1.md), [Lord Eerek](../characters/pcs/lord-eerek.md), [Strange](../characters/pcs/strange.md), and [Kronos](../characters/pcs/kronos.md) found out why the apples of [Bramblewick](../continents/faerun/cities/bramblewick/README.md) rotted, and jailed the false mayor.
- In [session-2026-11-08-dm1](../sessions/session-2026-11-08-dm1.md), four members stopped the Gobble-ins' wagon raids south of Neverwinter. [Cluckbane](../characters/npcs/cluckbane.md) hopes to join the Guard after a year of road-warden service, which is unrelated to the Guard.
- In [session-2024-11-17-dm2](../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm2.md), a Discord post says the Guard was rebuilding after a big dragon attack that ravaged its headquarters. When that happened, the dragon, and where the headquarters is are not recorded. Three members went to [Dis](../continents/hell/cities/dis/README.md) in Hell and rescued [Labor](../characters/npcs/labor.md) from the Mentiri, with a lead from [Lord Pezz](../characters/npcs/lord-pezz.md).
- In [session-2024-11-17-dm3](../sessions/session-2024-11-17-dm3.md), a Discord post says four members went upriver to [Godsbreath](../continents/faerun/cities/godsbreath/README.md) to find supplies and artisans for rebuilding the guild keep, the Guard's headquarters. They stopped a soul shaker, and the town delivered supplies and food and revived [Roland](../characters/pcs/roland.md).
- In [session-2024-11-24-dm1](../sessions/session-2024-11-24-dm1.md), an in-world report says Guard members attacked awakened serpent folk to clear Amaakos's escape route from the temple [Szessith's Embrace](../continents/faerun/locations/szessiths-embrace.md), home of the [Dark Embrace](dark-embrace.md). The session date was corrected from 11/1 to 11/24/2024.
- In [session-2024-12-01-dm3](../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm3.md), a Guard bulletin says members helped [Gratsdon](../continents/faerun/cities/gratsdon/README.md) prepare a halfling harvest festival at the request of the wizard [Fangil](../characters/npcs/fangil.md). It also warns members that the [soul shaker](../characters/npcs/godsbreath-soul-shaker.md) was met again outside Neverwinter, and to destroy all of its hands. Which members took part is not recorded.
- In [session-2024-12-01-dm4](../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm4.md), a report says four guild members returning from a job paid to enter [a maze](../continents/faerun/locations/the-maze.md) and were attacked by bean stalks. The report says only two survived, but the owner ruled retroactively that all four barely escaped. Whether the guild is the Amethyst Guard is taken as such.
- In [session-2024-12-01-dm2](../sessions/session-2024-12-01-dm2.md), a report says five members scouted a potential site for the new Amethyst Keep at the base of [Mount Hotenow](../continents/faerun/locations/mount-hotenow.md). They fought a bulette and lost [Lady Arka](../characters/pcs/lady-arka.md), who is dead for good. They freed a fire elemental and found an [electric bow](../items/electric-bow.md). Whether the site was chosen is not recorded.
- In [session-2025-03-02-dm3](../sessions/session-2025-03-02-dm3.md), a bulletin titled "Guard Betrayed" says members went to [Smelton](../continents/faerun/cities/smelton/README.md) to help protect its mine, and found that [Nolan](../characters/npcs/nolan.md), head of the town, had been corrupting the land and exploiting the miners. They ran him out and gave his wealth to the miners. The bulletin warns members to use discretion when accepting jobs, so they are not deceived into aiding evil. Which members took part is not recorded.
- In [session-2025-03-09-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-03-09-dm1.md), five members ended a string of burglaries in [Lumenshire](../continents/faerun/cities/lumenshire/README.md) by baiting a trap for the [Prismatic Gnome Rangers](prismatic-gnome-rangers.md). They captured the Green Ranger, who was held by the Guard and escaped before trial, and defeated all but the Pink Ranger.
- In [session-2025-04-13-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-04-13-dm1.md), five members rescued the brewmaster [Turdrag Hardbelt](../characters/npcs/turdrag-hardbelt.md) and the [Frosthaven Ale recipe](../items/frosthaven-ale-recipe.md) from the necromancer [Mortimer Graves](../characters/npcs/mortimer-graves.md) in the ruins of [Blackstone](../continents/faerun/locations/blackstone.md). The head of the [Amber Tun Brewers' Guild](amber-tun-brewers-guild.md) praised them.

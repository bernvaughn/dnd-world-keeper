# Prismatic Gnome Rangers

A band of five gnome burglars named for colors. Active in [Lumenshire](../continents/faerun/cities/lumenshire/README.md) until the Amethyst Guard stopped them in [session-2025-03-09-dm1](../sessions/session-2025-03-09-dm1.md).

## Members

All gnomes. Names beyond the colors are not recorded.

- [Green Ranger](../characters/npcs/green-ranger.md): captured by the Guard, then escaped before trial
- [Pink Ranger](../characters/npcs/pink-ranger.md): escaped, probably the one who put a contract on Ember
- Yellow Ranger: defeated
- Blue Ranger: defeated
- Red Ranger: defeated

Yellow, Blue, and Red have no files. Whether they died or were captured is not recorded.

## Notes

- Their methods were burglary of the vacation homes, and they ambushed the party to free the Green Ranger.

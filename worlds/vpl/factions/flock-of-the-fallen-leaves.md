# Flock of the Fallen Leaves

The Gobble-ins: a band of former robbers turned into giant, sentient turkeys after wishing on a genie's lamp to be "powerful and ruthless goblins." They settled in the woods south of Neverwinter, then returned to banditry to protect their home. Their second wish turned them into [gobble-lins](../creatures/gobble-lins.md), a creature that already existed, which is why they are the sentient version. Established by the owner after the sessions. Like the wild kind, they are horse-sized. The [lamp](../items/cracked-genie-lamp.md) is connected to [Zhudee the Grand](../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md).

## Members

Eight in total. Four took part in the wagon raids, and which four is not recorded beyond those named below.

- [Gobb the Red](../characters/npcs/gobb-the-red.md): flock leader, trying to turn away from robbery and protect the others
- [Cluckbane](../characters/npcs/cluckbane.md)
- [Pip](../characters/npcs/pip.md): lookout
- [Auntie Brindle](../characters/npcs/auntie-brindle.md): older, unarmed
- Mottle
- Sedge
- Tatter
- Crook

Mottle, Sedge, Tatter, and Crook have no files. Nothing else about them is recorded.

## History

Established by the owner after the sessions.

- The gang found [Zhudee the Grand](../characters/npcs/zhudee-the-grand.md)'s lamp in ruins and got three wishes: a feast, then to live as powerful goblins so they would never pay for food again, then to free the genie. [Pip](../characters/npcs/pip.md) convinced the flock to free him, and they threw the cracked lamp into a sinkhole for fun.

## Base

The [Gobble-in estate](../continents/faerun/locations/gobble-in-estate.md).
